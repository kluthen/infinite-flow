## Rules

- Motion communicates phrase replacement and immediate interaction feedback; it never decorates the music stand.
- Use `--duration-fade` with `--ease-out-gentle` for staff-content entrance after an initial or regenerated phrase is fully engraved.
- Fade old staff content with `--duration-fade-fast` before inserting a newly ready phrase. The staff frame, measure slots, control positions, and page layout do not move.
- Use `--duration-feedback` with `--ease-out-fast` for button and segmented-control feedback.
- Do not animate notes independently, scroll the staff, or delay an enabled next action. During a session the playhead steps from measure to measure at each barline, on the audio clock, and never glides; nothing else moves with the music.
- Anything that happens in time with the music changes instantly, with no transition or easing, because an eased change lands late by the length of its easing: the count-in's beats lighting, a note's acknowledgement colour, the playhead's step.
- Session refills use the phrase-replacement fades above: a system the playhead has left takes the `--duration-fade-fast` fade-out at once, and its new material enters with the `--duration-fade` entrance when engraved. Only the system not being read ever fades.
- The session cue row's contents (the starting-note line, the count-in, the notices, the phone's compact Stop and Volume) and the staff's start marker appear and leave with no animation, and the disconnect countdown's number changes with no animation. The row itself never changes size.
- **[v3 new]** The cue row's two links, `Review last session` and `All sessions`, appear and leave with no animation, and the starting-note line changes from its before-the-first-click wording to its usual one at once, on that first click.
- If the staff is out of view when a session starts, the page jumps to the top of the workspace instantly, never with a smooth scroll, once, before the count-in. At `bp-mobile-landscape` the same single instant jump instead settles the page so the session cue row and both staff systems are wholly in view. The app scrolls nothing else during a session.
- When a viewport change during a session leaves room for the staff, both systems re-engrave at the new size instantly, with no fade: a re-engrave is not a refill, and only a refill fades. It is the one change a session makes to the system being read, allowed because the player caused it by resizing the window. A browser toolbar hiding or reappearing on a phone redraws nothing: the phone's staff is sized to the height with the toolbars showing. A session start redraws nothing: phrase 1 is already shown in the layout the session keeps, on a phone as on a larger screen.
- The notice that replaces the score when the staff has no room (`room-notice`) appears and leaves with no animation, like the rotate-device message: it tracks the window's size, and a fade would flicker as a window is dragged across the limit. For the same reason its sentence, which depends on the breakpoint and on why the staff has no room, changes with no animation. When it clears, a new phrase is generated and enters with the ordinary loading and `--duration-fade` entrance; when the rhythm settings cannot form a phrase at that moment, it gives way to the empty staff instead, with no animation. Under the too-many-notes sentence, the round of retries that a widening, a change to a setting the phrase is generated from, or `Regenerate phrase` starts runs under the ordinary loading with the notice kept in place, never blinking off and back on; the first phrase that fits enters with the same entrance, and the notice leaves with no animation. A phrase regenerated at idle because it was too wide for the room uses the ordinary loading and entrance; its failed attempts are never drawn.
- The audio mix's Volume popover opens and closes instantly. The toggle buttons use the ordinary button feedback.
- The review of the last session (`review-panel`) takes the staff area's place at a session end, and gives it back on `Close review`, with no animation; `Review last session` shows it again the same way. Each switch puts the page at its top with an instant jump, never a smooth scroll, like the session start's jump. Its phrases are drawn as they come near the screen with no fade, since a fade on something scrolling into view is decoration and would flicker under a fast scroll. At a session end the staff's last systems are cleared out of sight, and the next phrase lands there with the ordinary loading, also out of sight; the player finds it on leaving the review.
- A value the app changes during a session (a setting varied by `Vary settings`, the tempo raised by `Rising tempo`) replaces the old one in the read-only control and in `tempo-readout` instantly, at the moment the first phrase generated with that setting, or playing at that tempo, starts sounding, with no transition, highlight or easing, and moves nothing. At the session end the controls return to the player's values just as instantly, out of sight under the review.
- In the review, the played-notes lanes appear and go with no animation when `Played notes` is switched, and are drawn with their phrase, with no fade, like the phrases themselves. The list grows or shrinks around the phrase in view, held in place by the browser's scroll anchoring; nothing scrolls on its own.
- **[v3 new]** The review mode's two levels, a session's review (`review-panel`) and the session list (`sessions-panel`), replace each other with no animation, either way, and each switch puts the page at its top with an instant jump, never a smooth scroll, as `Close review` and `Review last session` do. `All sessions` in the cue row opens the list the same way. A kept session's phrases are drawn as they come near the screen with no fade, like the last session's.
- **[v3 new]** A notice in the notice row (`app-notice`) appears and leaves with no animation. It changes only outside a session, so the workspace under it may move when one appears or is dismissed; it moves at once, never with a slide.
- **[v3 new]** The favorite toggle (`favorite-toggle`) changes state with the ordinary `--duration-feedback` button feedback and nothing else: no burst, no growing star. The keep line and the pin button it changes take their new words in place, with no animation.
- **[v3 new]** The session comment opens for editing, and closes again, with no animation, and its field grows with its text with no transition.
- **[v3 new]** The My instrument and Your data sheets, every confirmation shown as a sheet of its own, and the out-of-date phrase prompt open and close exactly as the connection sheet does: the `sheet-backdrop` and `sheet` opacity transition, `--duration-feedback` with `--ease-out-fast`, with no spatial motion. A confirmation shown inside a sheet already open replaces that sheet's content with no animation, as the connection sheet's own states do. The range refusal in the My instrument sheet (`range-alert`) and the derived register windows change with no animation as the steppers move.
- **[v3 new]** The instrument control's fourth choice, my instrument, appears when it is made and goes when it is deleted with no animation; the other segments rewrap at once. A saved clef change redraws the phrase on screen at once in the new clef, with no fade: its notes are the same, so it is not a phrase replacement.
- **[v3 new]** Nothing animates on a save, and nothing shows one: no spinner, no tick, no "saved" flash. Phrases prepared in the background between sessions show nothing either.
- **[v3.1 new]** Choosing a preset changes every control it sets at once, in place, with no highlight and no animation: the player's own choice is the cause, and a ripple of changes down the controls would only delay reading them. The preset box's list is the browser's own and opens as the browser opens it. The key and mode marks (`is-marked`) and their sentences appear and leave with no animation, and the Embellishments levels take the ordinary segment feedback. The preset sheet and the delete confirmation open and close as every sheet does; a refused name's reason (`name-alert`) appears and leaves with no animation as the name is typed.
- **[v3.2 new]** Switching between session mode and Jam mode replaces the staff area and the controls at once, with no fade, slide or cross-fade, and scrolls nothing: the player's own choice is the cause, and the two modes are not one phrase replacing another. While Jam mode's part of the app loads after the first press, the mode switch only dims, with the `wait` cursor: no spinner, no progress bar, nothing that would suggest a measured progress; its failure notice appears in the notice row with no animation, like every notice.
- **[v3.2 new]** In a Jam, the cue row's doodle measures light at each barline on the audio clock with no transition, like the count-in's beats; "Writing your first phrase" appears and leaves with no animation and nothing in it moves (no animated dots: it would suggest progress the app cannot measure). Phrase 1 enters the empty staff at the read-in measure with the ordinary `--duration-fade` entrance, and the empty staff with its instruction leaves with the `--duration-fade-fast` fade-out, as any refill does. The empty staff of Jam mode, its instruction and its notice, the kept doodle's bar, the line saying a Jam needs a controller and the key and mode suggestion appear and leave with no animation; `New doodle` gives the empty staff back at once. A phrase 1 written again from the kept doodle after a setting change enters with the ordinary loading and entrance. The options that appear under their switch (allowed rhythm changes, what may vary) appear and leave at once, the columns rewrapping with no transition. The review's doodle section is drawn like a phrase, with no fade.
- **[v3.2 new]** Tap-in changes the tempo stepper's value at once on each tap from the fourth, with no transition, counter roll or highlight, and `Tap tempo`'s listening border comes and goes with the ordinary `--duration-feedback` button feedback; no tap sounds or flashes anything. The readings of what the app took from the doodle fill and empty at once, out of the way below the staff, with no animation.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, staff replacement is instant and only non-spatial color feedback remains.
