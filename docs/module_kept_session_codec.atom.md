# Kept-Session Codec

## INTENT
Turn a finished session's in-memory history into the records a kept session is stored as, and those records back into exactly what the review and the exports read, so a kept session reviews and exports as it did when it ended.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
The codec is pure: it maps between values and never opens the database, reads a clock or draws a seed. Saving the records, choosing the session's key and deleting older sessions belong to whoever saves the session.

Encoding takes a finished session's in-memory history, with the session's facts the history does not hold (whether it is pinned, its favorites, its comment, whether its played notes are kept), and gives the session-wide record, the body and, only when the played notes are kept, the played-notes record. It reads the start time, the instrument id, the session seed, the phrases and their tempos from the history; whether the session is a Jam session, and, for a Jam session, its quantized doodle with its capture tempo and whether the player declined its suggestion; the preset label from the selection the history kept from the session's settings at its start (after Use it, the session's settings, not phrase 1's), written by the one preset-label function, and the instrument's display name from phrase 1's intent; the starting tempo from phrase 1 and the tempo reached from the last phrase; the length from phrase 1's scheduled start to the end of the last phrase. Each phrase's clef is copied from its history record, the clef the session's instrument was drawn in at the session's start (treble for a built-in instrument), never looked up from the instrument as it is now. The encoder throws, as a defect of the caller, for a history with no phrase, a phrase played on another instrument than the session's, a favorite that is not a phrase of the session or is listed twice, a comment over 500 characters or an empty app version. Every field is copied by name onto the stored shape, never by copying a history object whole, so the acknowledgements, the rhythm and tension provenance and the diagnostics the history holds never reach a record. Played notes are taken from every phrase, in onset order. Every time is rebased to seconds from phrase 1's scheduled start, but a fresh Jam's played notes, from its count-in's first beat, the body holding the doodle's first barline and phrase 1's start on that clock; a session without that clock holds no played note before phrase 1's start.

Decoding classifies each stored value through the record envelope first, each against its own shape's data version: 4 for the session-wide record and the body, 1 for the played notes. A newer record leaves the whole session unread and reported as newer, so a session saved by a newer version is listed as such and does not open. An unreadable value, or a current one that fails validation, makes the session unreadable, never repaired, defaulted or partly decoded. A current record is validated field by field: the generation request through its own parser, the timeline through the timeline parser, the clef as one of the instrument clefs, and the harmony and the generation intent through the codec's own validators, which check every field against the domain types and call the domain's parsers where they exist (the carry-in's among them). A phrase's intent may hold the rhythm lock group and the steering block, each read through the generation request's own parser for it, and a carry-in from the phrase before or, on phrase 1 beside a steering block only, from no phrase. A Jam session's quantized doodle is read through the doodle's own parser; a session that is not a Jam holds none. The intent's instrument profile goes through the profile parser on the data the record holds, never rebuilt from its id. The session-wide record's preset label is read as the text it holds. The intent's figure levels are read as stored; a stored intent holding a musical profile is refused, and a decoded intent holds none. A data version 1 session-wide record or body is below its shape's data version, so it reads as unreadable: the codec's data version 1 readers, which read a session-wide record's musical profile selection as the preset label it names and a phrase in any of data version 1's three shapes, a stored profile as that profile's figure levels (module_stored_profile_to_levels), serve only the upgrade step that carries those records over (module_kept_session_upgrade_step). Likewise, a data version 2 session-wide record or body is below its shape's data version and reads as unreadable: the codec's data version 2 readers, which read the shapes v3.1 wrote, serve only the step that carries those records over to data version 4 (module_kept_session_jam_upgrade_step). They refuse a stored request holding a rhythm lock group or a steering block, as v3.1's reader did. So a v3.0 session reviews and exports exactly as it did, its phrases' profile read as that profile's figure levels and its timelines kept as they were. Records that disagree are unreadable too: a request and an intent with another request id or seed, or that differ on whether they hold a rhythm lock group, on whether they hold a steering block, or on the origin of their carry-in (from a phrase or from no phrase), a body whose phrase count, favorites count or instrument differs from the session-wide record's, a played-notes record present when the played notes are not kept, or missing when they are, a session without a fresh Jam's clock whose played notes include one before phrase 1's start, or a clock whose phrase 1 does not start after the doodle's first barline.

Decoding gives what the review and the exports read for that session: its session-wide facts, whether it is a Jam session with, for one, its quantized doodle and capture tempo and whether its suggestion was declined, and its phrases in order, each with its number, start, outcome, intent, tempo, repeat mark, favorite mark and clef, and the played notes placed in the phrase their onset falls in, as the history places them, phrase 1 starting at time 0. The review and the exports read a phrase through a narrower type that a live history record and a decoded kept phrase both give: its number, start, outcome, intent, tempo, played notes and clef, the outcome holding only the result, with its request id, seed and timeline, and the harmony. Decoding fills no field the records do not hold: no acknowledgement, provenance or diagnostics is made up. A session decoded this way reviews and exports exactly as the same session did in memory when it ended.

The history and the export modules never import the codec, and stay free of storage.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
src/storage/records/kept-session-record.ts exports encodeKeptSession(history, facts, appVersion), from the history (KeptSessionHistory: its phrases, startedAt, instrumentProfileId, selection and sessionSeed) and the session's KeptSessionFacts (pinned, favorites, comment, playedNotesKept, and presets, the presets the session knew, the built-ins then the player's, by which the label names the selected preset, and, for a Jam session only, jam, its KeptJamFacts: the doodle (KeptDoodle: quantized and captureTempo), suggestionDeclined, and fresh, a fresh Jam's KeptFreshJam (countInStartsAt, doodleFirstBarlineAt and rawDoodleNotes on the audio clock), null for a Jam on a kept doodle) to KeptSessionRecords (summary, body, and playedNotes or null), and decodeKeptSession(stored), from the stored values (StoredKeptSession: summary, body, and playedNotes or undefined) to a KeptReading of a KeptSession (its KeptSessionSummary, its KeptPhrase list and its KeptJam, null for a session that is no Jam: the doodle, the played-notes clock, null for a Jam on a kept doodle, and the raw doodle notes) or a newer or unreadable report. decodeKeptSessionSummary reads a session-wide record alone. encodeKeptPlayedNotes(history, appVersion, fresh?) gives the played-notes record a session's save writes, for a change after the save that keeps its played notes; given a fresh Jam's moments, its raw doodle notes too and every time from its count-in's first beat. The work is split over kept-session-summary.ts (the session-wide record, isJam and, present exactly on a Jam session, suggestionDeclined among its keys), kept-session-body.ts (the body's shape, its doodle and playedNotesClock keys, held only by a Jam session's body, the clock only beside the doodle; its writers; and its earlier data versions' readers, which the upgrade steps run when the database opens), kept-session-body-read.ts (parseKeptSessionBody, the data version 4 body reader, with the Jam parts, KEPT_DOODLE_KEYS: quantized, captureTempo, and KEPT_PLAYED_NOTES_CLOCK_KEYS: doodleFirstBarline, phraseOneStart; only the codec imports it, so the start-up upgrade never loads the doodle's reader), kept-played-notes.ts (splitAtPhraseOne, which splits a fresh Jam's played notes at phrase 1's start on its clock), kept-reading.ts (the envelope classification through src/storage/record-envelope.ts, each shape's data version: KEPT_SESSION_SUMMARY_DATA_VERSION 4, KEPT_SESSION_BODY_DATA_VERSION 4, KEPT_PLAYED_NOTES_DATA_VERSION 1), kept-record-shape.ts (exact-key and value checks, a stored array with a hole refused, KeptRecordError), kept-phrase-parsers.ts (parseKeptHarmony, parseKeptProfileSelection, and isKeptPhraseRefusal, which tells a refusal of a stored value, the quantized doodle's errors of src/domain/doodle/quantized-doodle-errors.ts among them, from a defect) and kept-intent-parsers.ts (parseKeptIntent, parseKeptIntentV2, parseKeptIntentV1), with profile-to-levels.ts (module_stored_profile_to_levels) for a stored profile; a stored doodle is read through parseQuantizedDoodle (src/domain/doodle/quantized-doodle.ts); the preset label comes from presetLabel(selection, presets) in src/domain/controls/preset-label.ts, and the length from sessionLengthSeconds in src/session/session-timing.ts, the function the review gives the length with too. parseKeptIntent reads a data version 4 phrase's intent: its levels always, a musical profile refused, the rhythm lock group and the steering block when present, through the generation request's parsers, and a carry-in of either origin, from no phrase only beside a steering block. The request and the intent of a phrase must agree on the request id and the seed, and on whether they hold each group and on the carry-in's origin, presence only; a carry-in from no phrase is read on phrase 1 only. The data version 1 readers the upgrade step uses are parseKeptSessionSummaryV1 (kept-session-summary.ts), which reads the musical profile selection through parseKeptProfileSelection and gives its preset label, parseKeptSessionBodyV1 (kept-session-body.ts) and parseKeptIntentV1, which reads a phrase's levels in any of data version 1's three shapes: the levels only; a musical profile only, as v3.0 wrote it; or both, as written while the request held the two, where the stored levels must equal the profile's mapped levels, else the record is unreadable; rewrittenKeptSummaryRecordV2 and rewrittenKeptBodyRecordV2 write what they read at data version 2. The data version 2 readers the step to data version 4 uses are parseKeptSessionSummaryV2 (kept-session-summary.ts), parseKeptSessionBodyV2 (kept-session-body.ts), whose phrases' stored request is refused when it holds a rhythm lock group or a steering block, and parseKeptIntentV2, which reads v3.1's intent shape; rewrittenKeptSummaryRecord and rewrittenKeptBodyRecord write a read session-wide record and body at data version 4, for that step and for a change after the save. Validation uses parseGenerationRequest (src/domain/generation/generation-request.ts), the carry-in parsers of src/domain/generation/carry-in/carry-in.ts (parsePhraseCarryIn for a carry-in from a phrase, in v3.1's exact form, the only kind a data version 2 record holds; a carry-in from no phrase read only on phrase 1 beside a steering block), parseConcertEventTimeline (src/domain/events/timeline-parser.ts), parseInstrumentProfile (src/domain/profile/instrument-profile.ts) and isInstrumentClef (src/domain/profile/instrument-clef.ts); never rebuildGenerationWorkerIntent or parseGenerationWorkerIntent, which rebuild the profile from its id. The narrower phrase type, ReviewedPhrase with ReviewedOutcome, is declared in src/session/session-history.ts; it carries the phrase's clef, SessionHistoryRecord and KeptPhrase both extend it, and the review components (ReviewPhrase.vue drawing each phrase in record.clef) and the export adapters of src/session/session-history-export.ts (the MusicXML input in record.clef) take it; and notationInputFromOutcome (src/domain/notation/notation-input.ts) takes the matching NotatedOutcome. src/session/ and src/export/ do not import the codec; the history's storage trap test stays green.

## EXPECTATION
- A recorded five-phrase session with played notes and acknowledgements encodes to records whose keys are on the allowlist: no acknowledgements, provenance.rhythm, provenance.tensionBinding or diagnostics anywhere.
- Decoding gives a review input from which the MusicXML and MIDI exports are byte-identical to those of the in-memory session, for the three built-in instruments; the MusicXML export of a session played with my instrument is byte-identical too, in the clef it was played in.
- Played-note times decode relative to the start of their clock, the session's start or a fresh Jam's count-in, and export identically.
- A v3.0-shaped kept session, whose phrases hold a musical profile, reviews and exports exactly as it did, each phrase read with that profile's figure levels.
- A session with locked, changed and forced-bend phrases, and a Jam session whose phrase 1 holds its join from the doodle and its steering block, encode and decode with every reproduction input, and each phrase replays from them.
- A session kept by v3.1, carried over to data version 4, reviews and exports exactly as it did, with no Jam part.
- A data version 2 record read after the upgrade is unreadable; one above data version 4 is newer and left unread.
- A fresh Jam's records round-trip with its raw doodle notes before phrase 1 and its clock; a session without that clock holding a played note before phrase 1, or a clock whose phrase 1 does not start after the doodle's first barline, is unreadable.
- A kept phrase whose request holds a rhythm lock group, a steering block or a carry-in from no phrase that its intent lacks, or the reverse, makes the session unreadable; a data version 2 record holding either group is refused by the upgrade's readers and stays unreadable.
- After Use it, the session-wide record's preset label names the selection of the session's settings at its start, not phrase 1's.
- The session seed decodes to the value the history held, and the instrument to its id with the display name it had then.
- A newer record is reported as newer and not decoded; an invalid one, or records that disagree, as unreadable, never repaired.
- A stored harmony or intent with a missing, extra, ill-typed or out-of-range field is refused.
- A profile is decoded from the data the record holds, never rebuilt from its id.
- A session played with my instrument in bass clef encodes and decodes with its profile and the bass clef on every phrase, whatever clef my instrument has afterwards, and its review draws each phrase in that clef.
- The session history and the export modules stay storage-free.
