# Unsupported Rhythm Settings Give Way to No Phrase

## INTENT
Show no phrase, rather than a stale phrase or the room notice, when a replacement phrase is needed but the rhythm settings allow none.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
While the rhythm settings are a combination that blocks generation (judged on the note density a phrase would be generated with), no retry can run. In each case below, the phrase last on screen is not drawn again and the notation error is not shown: instead no phrase is shown, as before a first phrase exists, with the unsupported-rhythm alert; generation stays blocked until the combination is allowed again, by a setting change or by the window crossing the tablet-landscape layout's width; and once it is allowed again nothing is generated by itself: the player generates a phrase by hand.

1. The "staff needs more room" notice clears because the staff fits again while the combination blocks generation.
2. While the notice shows with its too-dense cause, a change that would start a retry round makes the combination one that blocks generation (a setting change, or the window getting wider past the tablet-landscape layout's width with a chosen Sixteenth): no round starts.
3. That same widening happens while a round runs: the round stops at once, and none of its attempts is drawn.
4. Outside the notice, a phrase on screen stops fitting the staff while the combination blocks generation: it is not drawn, gets no retry, and does not take the notice, whose cures cannot work while no phrase can be generated. A window with no room for the staff at all still shows the notice, which is about the window; when it clears, case 1 applies.

A phrase on screen when the combination becomes one that blocks generation otherwise stays on screen, with the alert, as in the first release, as long as it still fits.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
Under a blocked rhythm-settings combination, the staff area shows no phrase with the unsupported-rhythm alert whenever a replacement phrase would be needed.

## EXPECTATION
- In each of the four cases, no phrase is generated or shown, the unsupported-rhythm alert shows, and no retry runs.
- Once the combination is allowed again, no phrase is generated until the player asks for one.
- Neither the phrase last on screen nor any attempt of a stopped round is ever drawn, and the notation error never appears in these cases.
