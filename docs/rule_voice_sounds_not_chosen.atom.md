# No Setting Chooses a Voice's Sound

## INTENT
Keep the choice of instrument and sound out of the app for now, while keeping the door open.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
Every system voice, the backing's bass and chords, the click, the two guides and each part, has one sound the app chooses. No setting shows or chooses an instrument or a sound for any of them.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
No control lists or chooses an instrument or a sound.

## EXPECTATION
- No setting shows or changes the sound of any system voice.
