# Session Engine

## INTENT

Decide, as a pure and deterministic state machine that never reads a clock, what a running session requests, shows and ends, from timed session events alone.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
The engine is a stateful closure over immutable state, with no browser API, no Vue and no clock read. Time is audio time: plain seconds carried on every event. Its only injected dependencies are the request-identity and seed providers, a window-width reading that tells whether the window is below the tablet-landscape layout's width, and a display reading that tells whether any of a given phrase is on screen. The same event list therefore always yields the same outputs. Every output is deep-frozen, and attempt ids are unique per engine, increasing from 1.

- Start takes the start time, the chosen settings (controls and musical profile, deep-frozen at start), phrase 1 (the Idle phrase's intent and successful outcome; any other outcome throws) and the settings-mutation choice: off, or on with the session's mutation seed, which the caller draws and stores, so a session replays from its start and its event list alone. Start also says whether adaptive tempo runs: only when the page-lifetime adaptive-tempo flag is on and the session has a controller at its start (a session started with the selected controller away has none). It says too whether the multi-phrase tension arc runs, a boolean checked at start (anything else throws) and fixed for the session. The settings in effect are part of the engine state, starting as the chosen settings; every intent of the session is built from the settings in effect when its request is built, as its phrase is generated under them (see Multi-phrase tension arc).
- Events, each with its time: count-in over; phrase started sounding (phrase 2 onward); generation result (attempt id, outcome); generation crashed (attempt id: the generate request was rejected, by a worker crash on that request or by a dead worker); fit result (attempt id, fits); settings change (the complete settings in effect from then on, from an automatic source other than the engine's own mutations); controller (attached or not: a controller lost mid-session going away, or reconnecting); tick; end (cause: stop, hidden tab, staff not shown, silence timeout, controller lost).
- Outputs: request generation (attempt id, phrase number, step carry-in or standalone, retry, intent, deadline), where retry counts the attempts made for that phrase before this one: 0 for the first carry-in attempt, 1 to 3 for the carry-in retries, 4 for the standalone retry; check fit (attempt id, phrase number, outcome, and the intent of the attempt being checked); phrase ready (the phrase record to show next as read-ahead, repeats included); phrase sounding (time, phrase record and that phrase's tempo); struggling notice (visible or not); wake at (a deadline, output once per deadline when its phrase is requested, not once per retry, since every attempt of one phrase shares that deadline); ended (time, cause, phase count-in or running, number of phrases that sounded); settings changed (the first phrase number the change applies to, the settings in effect from that phrase, and its cause: a mutation, which also names the one setting it stepped and the values it stepped from and to, or an automatic change); phrase discarded (the number of a decided look-ahead phrase, already output as phrase ready, dropped to be requested again with new settings); phrase tempo (the number of a phrase from 2 on and the tempo it plays at, output when that tempo is fixed).
- Phrase record: phrase number, origin (idle, carry-in, standalone or repeat), intent, outcome, and for a repeat the number of the phrase directly before it, which is the phrase repeated, even when that phrase is itself a repeat. The repeat origin is the internal repeat mark. Phrase 1 gets no phrase ready, since it is already on screen from Idle; it appears only as the phrase sounding at count-in over.

Timing. A measure is four beats at the tempo of the phrase it belongs to; phrase 1 plays at the starting tempo, the tempo of the chosen settings. Count-in over is the moment phrase 1 starts sounding; the count-in is one measure at the starting tempo. Phrase 2 is requested at start with its deadline at the count-in's end, and count-in over requests nothing new. When phrase N (N of 2 or more) starts sounding, phrase N+1 is requested with its deadline at the start of phrase N's last measure, computed from phrase N's own tempo. Every event except end first runs the deadline decision for its time; a result arriving at or after the deadline is too late. End runs no deadline decision, so a session that is ending never decides one more phrase (a repeat) that would never be shown. Tempo only sets these times, never an intent.

Failure ladder. One attempt per look-ahead phrase is in flight at a time. The first attempt carries in from the phrase before, with a fresh seed. A failed attempt (a generation that found no phrase, a rejected generate request, whether a worker crash or a dead worker, or a phrase that does not fit) leads to up to three retries with a fresh seed and the same carry-in, requested only before the deadline, then one standalone retry with no carry-in and a fresh seed, its register fixed to the session's resolved register window so a Varying register stays resolved once per session. A successful outcome becomes ready only after its fit check passes. If no valid phrase exists at the deadline, whether the ladder is exhausted or an attempt is still in flight, the previous phrase is repeated as the next one, decided at the deadline and not earlier. The phrase after a standalone or a repeat carries in normally from that record. Results for an unknown or superseded attempt, or arriving after the deadline or the end, are dropped, since the worker cannot cancel a request; after the end, ticks and controller events are dropped too. No failure ever ends the session.

Caller errors. An event the engine cannot accept throws a typed session engine error rather than being hidden: a settings change that alters the instrument or the tempo; phrase started sounding before the count-in is over or for any phrase but the next decided one; a second count-in over; a time that goes backwards or is not finite; a second generation result, or a crash, for an attempt already waiting for its fit check; a fit result before the attempt's generation result; a controller event that does not change the controller's presence (away while already away, or back while attached); after the end, any event other than a late result, a tick or a controller event.

Struggling notice. Repeats are counted consecutively in decision order. The notice becomes visible when the second repeat in a row is decided, at its deadline, and stays through further repeats. A generated phrase, carry-in or standalone, resets the count, and the notice clears when that phrase starts sounding. It clears at the end.

Density. Each request's note density is resolved when the request is built, from the density in the settings in effect, the window-width reading at that moment and the session's step-down memory. The memory is set as soon as any attempt's request is built with Eighth for a chosen Sixteenth, whether or not that attempt succeeds, and holds until the session ends. Phrase 1 never sets it; phrase 2's request is built at start from the window then.

Settings mutation. With mutation on, the engine schedules and decides every mutation itself, through the pure settings-mutation module: mutation k lands on phrase p(k), the first at 1 plus the first gap and each later one a further gap on, each gap drawn from 2 to 4 from the mutation seed and k. Mutation k is decided when phrase p(k) - 1 starts sounding, just before phrase p(k) is requested, from the settings in effect, the density the session generates with, the resolved register, and whether Sixteenth is unavailable (window below the tablet-landscape layout's width, or the step-down memory set). It becomes the settings in effect at once and is output as settings changed for phrase p(k), so no look-ahead phrase is ever discarded for a mutation. If phrase p(k) ends up a repeat, the mutation still counts as applied at p(k), and the next generated phrase uses it. With mutation off, the settings in effect change only through a settings change event.

Adaptive tempo. Each phrase from 2 on has its tempo fixed at its look-ahead deadline (phrase 2's at the count-in's end), the point after which the decided phrase can no longer be discarded, and output then as phrase tempo: audio must be scheduled ahead, so this is the latest safe moment. Without adaptive tempo every phrase plays at the starting tempo. With it, the schedule is decided by a pure adaptive-tempo module the engine calls through thin glue. A controller event tells the engine when a controller lost mid-session goes away and when it reconnects; the controller is attached at start. The engine follows it whether or not adaptive tempo runs, during the count-in and while running; one that does not change the controller's presence throws, and one arriving after the end is dropped, since a loss can race the end. A phrase counts toward the next rise when it starts sounding (at count-in over for phrase 1, at phrase started sounding from phrase 2) while the controller is attached. Once two counted phrases have started since the last rise, the next phrase whose tempo is fixed while the controller is attached plays 5 BPM faster than the phrase before it, never above 240 BPM, and the count restarts at 0. Once a phrase plays at 240 BPM nothing changes any more: every later phrase plays at 240 BPM and no further rise happens. While the controller is away no rise happens and the due rise waits; a loss in the final measure before a phrase boundary cannot stop that boundary's rise, since that phrase's tempo is already fixed. Tempo is never part of the settings in effect: the settings and every intent keep the starting tempo, so generation and replay keys are unchanged, and a rise is never output as settings changed.

Multi-phrase tension arc. Each look-ahead phrase is generated under the settings the pure tension-arc cycle module gives from the flag, its own phrase number and the settings in effect after any mutation landing on it: with the flag on, its tension arc takes that phrase's four-phrase cycle step; with it off, or when the clamped step changes nothing, they are the settings in effect themselves. They are applied again, by the same phrase number, when a settings change requests a pending phrase again. The step never reaches the settings in effect, a mutation's draw or any settings changed output.

Settings change event. A settings change replaces the settings in effect at its time, even when it equals them: no change is treated as a special case. A decided phrase keeps its look-ahead, with its deadline, until it starts sounding. A look-ahead phrase not yet decided, or decided but not yet sounding, is dropped and requested again from the first rung with the new settings, from the same phrase before and keeping its deadline, only if none of it is on screen and at least one measure, at the tempo of the phrase sounding then (the starting tempo during the count-in), remains before its deadline; the display is read only when the time allows the drop. An attempt still in flight for an undecided phrase is superseded. A decided phrase is taken back as if never decided: the phrase before is the latest decided again, with the settings it was generated under, the struggling notice is as it was before that decision, and the drop is output as phrase discarded. Otherwise the phrase stays as it is and the change applies from the phrase after it. Settings changed names the first phrase the change applies to.

Carry-in after a change. Every look-ahead phrase carries a basis that all its attempts are built from: the settings it is generated under (the settings in effect when it was opened, with its tension-arc cycle step), the settings its phrase before was generated under, its step included (for a repeat, those of the phrase it repeats), and its source, either the carry-in, as adapted to any change between the two, or a standalone generation with a given register. When neither the register nor a setting the harmony check reads (tonal center, collection, harmonic motion, tension, musical profile) changed between the two, the carry-in is used as it is. With a standalone source, every rung of the ladder, the first included, is a standalone attempt with that register, keeping the rung's retry count, and an accepted phrase's origin is the step its attempt actually took. The first phrase requested under changed settings gets a re-checked carry-in. Its harmonic part is dropped after a key or collection change, or when the final harmony no longer fits the new harmony settings. A register change to Low, Middle or High replaces the carried window with the new register's resolved window. The pitch part is then re-checked against the new range and register window and, for an owed leap recovery, the new collection; if it fails, the request is built standalone, its register window pinned to the session's window after the change, as the ladder's standalone retry is. A change to Varying drops the carry-in, and the request is built standalone with Varying, so the window is resolved again as for phrase 1.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
`createSessionEngine(dependencies: SessionEngineDependencies, start: SessionStart)` in `src/session/session-engine.ts`, called as `createSessionEngine({ providers, viewport, display }, { at, settings, phraseOne, mutation, adaptiveTempo, multiPhraseTensionArc })`, where `providers` are the request-id and seed providers, `viewport` exposes only `isBelowTabletLandscape()`, `display` is a `SessionDisplayReading` exposing only `isPhraseOnScreen(phraseNumber): boolean`, and the `SessionStart` field `mutation` is `{ kind: 'off' } | { kind: 'on', seed }`, the field `adaptiveTempo: boolean` says whether adaptive tempo runs, and the field `multiPhraseTensionArc: boolean` says whether the multi-phrase tension arc runs, checked at start and kept in `EngineContext`. It returns `{ outputsAtStart, handle(event) }`, and `handle` returns the outputs of that one event; no state is exposed. Events: `count-in-over`, `phrase-started-sounding { phraseNumber }`, `generation-result { attemptId, outcome }`, `generation-crashed { attemptId }`, `fit-result { attemptId, fits }`, `settings-change { settings }`, `controller { attached: boolean }`, `tick`, `end { cause }` with cause `'stop' | 'hidden-tab' | 'staff-not-shown' | 'silence-timeout' | 'controller-lost'`, each with `at`. Outputs: `request-generation { attemptId, phraseNumber, step, retry, intent, deadline }`, `check-fit { attemptId, phraseNumber, outcome, intent }`, `phrase-ready`, `phrase-sounding` (carrying that phrase's `tempo`), `struggling-notice`, `wake-at`, `ended { at, cause, phase, soundedPhraseCount }`, `settings-changed`, either `{ phraseNumber, settings, cause: 'mutation', setting, from, to }` or `{ phraseNumber, settings, cause: 'automatic' }`, `phrase-discarded { phraseNumber }` and `phrase-tempo { phraseNumber, tempo }`. The phrase record is `SessionPhrase { phraseNumber, origin, intent, outcome, repeatOf? }`. A refused event throws `SessionEngineError`. The fit check is a seam the engine asks for through `check-fit`, never computes. Whether a phrase is on screen is a seam too, read through `display`, never computed by the engine. The engine's state and its shared transitions live in `src/session/session-engine-state.ts`, the settings change handling (look-ahead basis, attempt plan per rung, accepted change, discard decision) in `src/session/session-settings-change.ts`, the carry-in adaptation `adaptCarryIn` in `src/session/carry-in-adaptation.ts`, the mutation landing on a phrase about to be requested in `src/session/session-mutation-step.ts`, the adaptive-tempo schedule (counting and rises), a pure module, in `src/session/adaptive-tempo.ts` (`startAdaptiveTempo`, `controllerPresence`, `phraseStartedSounding`, `nextPhraseTempo`), and the engine's thin glue to it in `src/session/session-tempo-step.ts` (the tempo state at start, fixing a phrase's tempo once its deadline is reached, counting a sounding phrase, the controller event, and the tempo of a given or the sounding phrase), the tempo state `SessionTempo { schedule, fixed }` being kept in the engine state apart from the settings in effect, and the settings a phrase is generated under from `settingsGeneratedUnder` in `src/session/tension-arc-cycle.ts`, becoming its look-ahead basis's settings and, once decided, the engine state's `lastDecidedSettings`.

## EXPECTATION
- Driven by the same timed event list, the engine returns the same, deep-frozen outputs every time, with attempt ids increasing from 1, and never reads a clock.
- At 60, 120 and 240 BPM, phrase 2 is requested at start with its deadline at the count-in's end, and phrase N+1 is requested when phrase N starts sounding with its deadline at the start of N's last measure; every attempt of one phrase shares that deadline, and the request intents are identical at every tempo.
- Phrase 1 is never output as phrase ready; it is output as sounding at count-in over.
- Mixed generation failures, crashes and failed fit checks run up to three carry-in retries, then one standalone retry keeping the resolved register, before the deadline; with no valid phrase at the deadline the previous phrase is repeated, marked as a repeat of the phrase directly before, and the session keeps running.
- A late or superseded result never becomes a phrase.
- The struggling notice shows when the second consecutive repeat is decided, stays through further repeats, clears when a generated phrase starts sounding, and clears at the end.
- Once any request is built with Eighth for a chosen Sixteenth, every later request of the session uses Eighth.
- An end during the count-in reports the count-in phase and no sounded phrase; after the end, late results and ticks are dropped and any other event throws.
- Each event the engine cannot accept throws a session engine error.
- With mutation on, the same start (mutation seed included) and event list give the same mutations on the same phrase numbers, each output as settings changed for its landing phrase, and no phrase is discarded for a mutation; with mutation off, the settings in effect change only through a settings change event.
- A settings change drops and requests again a waiting look-ahead phrase, with its deadline unchanged, only when none of it is on screen and at least one measure remains before its deadline; otherwise the change applies from the phrase after it.
- Only a dropped phrase that was already decided is output as phrase discarded; its decision is taken back, the phrase before and the struggling notice returning to what they were before it.
- A settings change that alters the instrument or the tempo throws a session engine error.
- Each phrase from 2 on has its tempo fixed and output as phrase tempo at its look-ahead deadline, phrase sounding carries that phrase's tempo, and phrase N+1's deadline is computed from phrase N's own tempo; without adaptive tempo every phrase plays at the starting tempo.
- With adaptive tempo, once two phrases that started sounding while the controller was attached have started since the last rise, the next phrase whose tempo is fixed while it is attached plays 5 BPM faster, never above 240 BPM; while a controller lost mid-session is away no phrase counts and no rise happens.
- Once a phrase plays at 240 BPM, every later phrase plays at 240 BPM.
- The controller event is accepted with or without adaptive tempo, during the count-in and while running; one that does not change the controller's presence throws, and one after the end is dropped.
- With adaptive tempo, the settings in effect and every request intent keep the starting tempo, and no rise is output as settings changed.
- After a change, the next requested phrase carries in without harmony after a key or collection change or an unfit final harmony, with the new register's window after a register change, and is standalone in the session's current window when the pitch re-check fails, or standalone with Varying after a change to Varying.
