# Session Engine

## INTENT

Decide, as a pure and deterministic state machine that never reads a clock, what a running session requests, shows and ends, from timed session events alone.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
The engine is a stateful closure over immutable state, with no browser API, no Vue and no clock read. Time is audio time: plain seconds carried on every event. Its only injected dependencies are the request-identity provider, a window-width reading that tells whether the window is below the tablet-landscape layout's width, and a display reading that tells whether any of a given phrase is on screen. The same event list therefore always yields the same outputs. Every output is deep-frozen, and attempt ids are unique per engine, increasing from 1; a skipped ladder rung creates no attempt, but its request id is still drawn from the provider and discarded. The engine draws no seed: each attempt's seed is the one the session seed plan derives from the start's session seed for that attempt's phrase and ladder rung, so a skipped rung's seed is simply unused. Whether the standalone attempt is due is read from the event's own time against the phrase's deadline, never from a clock, and after every event is handled the engine runs the failure ladder's cutover for the open look-ahead phrase, its outputs joining that event's outputs.

- Start takes the start time, the chosen settings (controls, figure levels, preset selection, the restriction in force at Start when there is one, and, exactly when the controls name my instrument, my instrument's profile as data, taken at Start from phrase 1's intent, which embeds it; deep-frozen at start, settings whose profile does not match their instrument being refused), phrase 1 (the Idle phrase's intent and successful outcome; any other outcome throws), the settings phrase 1 was generated under (its controls, figure levels and preset selection as the Idle result recorded them, with the session's tempo and instrument; deep-frozen at start; absent, the chosen settings; ones with another tempo or instrument than the session's being refused), the session seed drawn with that phrase 1 at Idle, and the settings-mutation choice: off, or on with the session's mutation seed, which the caller derives from the session seed by the session seed plan and stores, so a session replays from its start and its event list alone. Start also says whether adaptive tempo runs: only when the page-lifetime adaptive-tempo flag is on and the session has a controller at its start (a session started with the selected controller away has none). It says too whether the multi-phrase tension arc runs, a boolean checked at start (anything else throws) and fixed for the session. It also carries the ready phrases, the phrases pre-generated at Idle that Start took, in order from phrase 2, at most four, each with its phrase number, ladder rung, intent and successful outcome (none when pre-generation is off or nothing is ready), checked at start; when a look-ahead opens on a phrase that has one, the engine re-derives its intent and, when it is identical, decides that phrase at once in place of requesting it ([[module_session_ready_phrases]]), so the same start and event list still give the same outputs. The settings in effect are part of the engine state, starting as the chosen settings. Phrase 1 is taken as decided under its own settings, never assumed to have been generated under the chosen settings: phrase 2's look-ahead basis has phrase 1's settings as the settings its phrase before was generated under, so its carry-in from phrase 1 is adapted to any difference between the two exactly as after a settings change applied at the boundary between phrase 1 and phrase 2 ([[module_session_carry_in_after_change]]), and no carry-in error can arise from that difference at any tempo. When phrase 1's settings equal the chosen settings, as for every phrase 1 that is not stale, nothing is adapted and the requests and seeds are the same as before. Every intent of the session is built from the settings in effect when its request is built, as its phrase is generated under them (with the multi-phrase tension arc on, the settings in effect with that phrase's tension-arc cycle step applied). Every attempt intent, carry-in and standalone alike, carries the settings' instrument profile, my instrument's as data and a built-in's resolved from its id, so a built-in session builds the same intents as before; the carry-in receiver converts the written tonal center with that profile, and every settings change, a Vary settings mutation included, keeps it.
- Events, each with its time: count-in over; phrase started sounding (phrase 2 onward); generation result (attempt id, outcome); generation crashed (attempt id: the generate request was rejected, by a worker crash on that request or by a dead worker); fit result (attempt id, fits); settings change (the complete settings in effect from then on, from an automatic source other than the engine's own mutations); controller (attached or not: a controller lost mid-session going away, or reconnecting); tick; end (cause: stop, hidden tab, staff not shown, silence timeout, controller lost).
- Outputs: request generation (attempt id, phrase number, step carry-in or standalone, retry, intent, deadline), where retry is the attempt's rung on the failure ladder: 0 for the first carry-in attempt, 1 to 3 for the carry-in retries, 4 for the standalone retry, also when the carry-in retries were skipped; check fit (attempt id, phrase number, outcome, and the intent of the attempt being checked); phrase ready (the phrase record to show next as read-ahead, repeats included); phrase sounding (time, phrase record and that phrase's tempo); struggling notice (visible or not); wake at (a time the engine must be woken at: a deadline, output once per deadline when its phrase is requested, not once per retry, since every attempt of one phrase shares that deadline; and, with every carry-in request, that phrase's cutover time, the deadline minus the standalone reserve, at which a carry-in attempt still generating gives way to the standalone attempt); ended (time, cause, phase count-in or running, number of phrases that sounded); settings changed (the first phrase number the change applies to, the settings in effect from that phrase, and its cause: a mutation, which also names the one setting it stepped and the values it stepped from and to, or an automatic change); phrase discarded (the number of a decided look-ahead phrase, already output as phrase ready, dropped to be requested again with new settings); phrase tempo (the number of a phrase from 2 on and the tempo it plays at, output when that tempo is fixed); cancel generation (the attempt id of an attempt the engine drops while it is still in its generating stage, so a later request never waits behind it on the generator: a carry-in attempt still generating at its phrase's cutover time, the attempt in flight when the deadline decides a repeat, the attempt of a look-ahead phrase a settings change drops to request again, and the attempt in flight when the session ends; within one event's outputs it comes before any request generation. An attempt in its fitting stage, whose generation result has arrived and which waits for its fit check, is never cancelled, since its generation is done; its fit result is dropped like any other late result).
- Phrase record: phrase number, origin (idle, carry-in, standalone or repeat), intent, outcome, and for a repeat the number of the phrase directly before it, which is the phrase repeated, even when that phrase is itself a repeat. The repeat origin is the internal repeat mark. Phrase 1 gets no phrase ready, since it is already on screen from Idle; it appears only as the phrase sounding at count-in over.

Caller errors. An event the engine cannot accept throws a typed session engine error rather than being hidden: a settings change that alters the instrument or the tempo; phrase started sounding before the count-in is over or for any phrase but the next decided one; a second count-in over; a time that goes backwards or is not finite; a second generation result, or a crash, for an attempt already waiting for its fit check; a fit result before the attempt's generation result; a controller event that does not change the controller's presence (away while already away, or back while attached); after the end, any event other than a late result, a tick or a controller event.

Parts. The engine's timing, its failure ladder, the struggling notice, density, settings mutation, adaptive tempo, the multi-phrase tension arc, the settings change event, the carry-in after a change and the ready phrases taken from Idle each have their own child atom: [[module_session_look_ahead_timing]], [[module_session_failure_ladder]], [[module_session_struggling_notice]], [[module_session_density_step_down]], [[module_session_mutation_step]], [[module_session_adaptive_tempo_schedule]], [[module_session_tension_arc_step]], [[module_session_settings_change]], [[module_session_carry_in_after_change]] and [[module_session_ready_phrases]].

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
`createSessionEngine(dependencies: SessionEngineDependencies, start: SessionStart)` in `src/session/session-engine.ts`, called as `createSessionEngine({ providers, viewport, display }, { at, settings, phraseOne, phraseOneSettings, sessionSeed, mutation, adaptiveTempo, multiPhraseTensionArc, readyPhrases })`, where `providers` holds only the request-id provider (`nextRequestId`), `viewport` exposes only `isBelowTabletLandscape()`, `display` is a `SessionDisplayReading` exposing only `isPhraseOnScreen(phraseNumber): boolean`, the optional `SessionStart` field `phraseOneSettings` holds the settings phrase 1 was generated under, built by `phraseOneSettings(recorded, phraseOneIntent, tempo)` and checked by `phraseOneSettingsAtStart` in `src/session/stale-phrase-one.ts` (absent, the chosen settings; another tempo or instrument than the session's throws `SessionEngineError`), which the engine state takes as `lastDecidedSettings` at start in place of the chosen settings, so `lookAheadBasis` in `src/session/session-settings-change.ts` adapts phrase 2's carry-in through `adaptCarryIn`; the `SessionStart` field `sessionSeed` is the session seed each attempt's seed derives from (`buildAttemptIntent` in `src/session/session-intents.ts`, through `phraseAttemptSeed`), the field `mutation` is `{ kind: 'off' } | { kind: 'on', seed }`, the field `adaptiveTempo: boolean` says whether adaptive tempo runs, the field `multiPhraseTensionArc: boolean` says whether the multi-phrase tension arc runs, checked at start and kept in `EngineContext`, and the optional field `readyPhrases` holds the phrases pre-generated at Idle, absent meaning none, checked by `requireReadyPhrases` and kept in `EngineContext`; a look-ahead that opens on a ready phrase goes through `takeReadyPhrase` (`src/session/session-ready-phrases.ts`) instead of `requestFirstRung`. It returns `{ outputsAtStart, handle(event) }`, and `handle` returns the outputs of that one event; no state is exposed. Events: `count-in-over`, `phrase-started-sounding { phraseNumber }`, `generation-result { attemptId, outcome }`, `generation-crashed { attemptId }`, `fit-result { attemptId, fits }`, `settings-change { settings }`, `controller { attached: boolean }`, `tick`, `end { cause }` with cause `'stop' | 'hidden-tab' | 'staff-not-shown' | 'silence-timeout' | 'controller-lost'`, each with `at`. Outputs: `request-generation { attemptId, phraseNumber, step, retry, intent, deadline }`, `check-fit { attemptId, phraseNumber, outcome, intent }`, `phrase-ready`, `phrase-sounding` (carrying that phrase's `tempo`), `struggling-notice`, `wake-at`, `ended { at, cause, phase, soundedPhraseCount }`, `settings-changed`, either `{ phraseNumber, settings, cause: 'mutation', setting, figure?, from, to }` or `{ phraseNumber, settings, cause: 'automatic' }`, `phrase-discarded { phraseNumber }`, `phrase-tempo { phraseNumber, tempo }` and `cancel-generation { attemptId }`, the last ordered before any `request-generation` in the same batch. Every carry-in `request-generation` is accompanied by a `wake-at` at its phrase's cutover time; the first rung goes through `firstRung` both in `requestNextPhrase` and when a settings change supersedes a look-ahead phrase, and `runCutover` runs after each event's dispatch. The phrase record is `SessionPhrase { phraseNumber, origin, intent, outcome, repeatOf?, relock? }`, `relock` the internal re-lock mark of module_session_rhythm_lock. The settings are `SessionSettings { controls, figureLevels, selection, instrumentProfile?, restriction?, allowedGroups, rhythmLockOn, allowedRhythmChanges }` (`src/session/session-types.ts`), `instrumentProfile` present exactly when the controls name `my-instrument`, `restriction` the restriction in force at Start (`withRestrictionAtStart`, module_settings_mutation), absent when none is in force or it allows every key and mode; `sessionSettingsAtStart(chosen, phraseOneIntent)` builds them at Start and `sessionInstrumentProfile(settings)` gives the settings' profile (`src/session/session-intents.ts`), which `buildAttemptIntent` passes to every attempt and the carry-in receiver (`receiverFor` in `src/session/carry-in-adaptation.ts`) converts with. A refused event throws `SessionEngineError`. The fit check is a seam the engine asks for through `check-fit`, never computes. Whether a phrase is on screen is a seam too, read through `display`, never computed by the engine. The engine's state and its shared transitions live in `src/session/session-engine-state.ts`, and its failure-ladder steps (`requestFirstRung`, `climb`, `runCutover`, `cancelDropped`) in `src/session/session-ladder-step.ts`.

## EXPECTATION
- Driven by the same timed event list, the engine returns the same, deep-frozen outputs every time, with attempt ids increasing from 1, and never reads a clock.
- Phrase 1 is never output as phrase ready; it is output as sounding at count-in over.
- An end during the count-in reports the count-in phase and no sounded phrase; after the end, late results and ticks are dropped and any other event throws.
- Each event the engine cannot accept throws a session engine error.
- A settings change that alters the instrument or the tempo throws a session engine error.
- Given settings with my instrument's profile, every attempt intent, carry-in and standalone alike, carries that profile and the carry-in receiver converts the tonal center with its transposition, nothing throwing; settings naming my instrument without its profile, or a built-in with one, are refused at start; a built-in session builds the same intents as before.
- A carry-in attempt still generating at its phrase's cutover time, or any attempt still generating when the deadline decides a repeat, when a settings change drops its look-ahead phrase, or when the session ends, is output as cancel generation, before any request generation of the same event; an attempt waiting for its fit check is never output as cancel generation.
- Every carry-in request generation is accompanied by a wake-at at its phrase's cutover time.
- Started with phrase 1's settings different from the chosen settings (a changed Written key, Collection, Harmonic motion or Register), the engine requests phrase 2 with its carry-in adapted as after a settings change at the boundary between phrase 1 and phrase 2, and no carry-in error is thrown at any tempo; started with the two equal, its outputs are exactly those it gave before phrase 1's settings were carried.
- Started with ready phrases, the engine decides each one whose re-derived intent is identical as phrase ready when its look-ahead opens, with no request generation, and drops the first that differs with every later one; started with none, its outputs are exactly those it gave before ready phrases existed.
