## What v3.3 is

Until v3.3 every chord the app writes is a [**triad**](../music/lexicon.md#triad): three notes
stacked in thirds, such as C–E–G. v3.3 adds an optional
[**seventh chord**](../music/lexicon.md#seventh-chord): a triad plus the note a seventh above its
[root](../music/lexicon.md#root) (the note the chord is built on and named after), such as
C–E–G–B, Cmaj7 ([master spec §D1](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#d1-core-concept)). The
guiding principle is unchanged: the app acknowledges and encourages, and never grades or scores the
player.

- **The Seventh chords level.** A setting at Idle, after Harmonic motion, with four values labelled
  **Off, Sometimes, Often and Always** (the spec's Off, Sometimes, Favored and Strongly favored; the
  labels are ux D485's). Off is the default. It says how often a chord is written in its seventh
  form: about 1 in 4 of the chords at Sometimes, about 1 in 2 at Often, every chord at Always. It is
  never a quota: a phrase that works only as triads still comes. The level is locked during a
  session, saved, and held by every preset, every built-in at Off; changing it makes Custom.
- **Jam's own level.** Jam mode has its own Seventh chords level, with the same four values, Off by
  default, set at Jam's Idle. It never changes session mode's level, nor the reverse. Changing it
  writes phrase 1 again from the kept doodle.
- **Only the four diatonic sevenths.** A chord's seventh form is the seventh chord on its own degree
  of the selected key and mode, so it uses only that mode's notes
  ([diatonic](../music/lexicon.md#diatonic)). There are four qualities: major seventh (maj7), minor
  seventh (m7), dominant seventh (7) and half-diminished seventh (m7♭5). The chord plans are Off's;
  only the forms differ.
- **The seventh as a chord tone.** The melody may use the seventh as it uses the triad's notes,
  runs staying three notes long. A phrase still never ends on its final chord's seventh (the triad
  reading).
- **Where the sevenths show.** Chord symbols (maj7, m7, 7, m7♭5) in written pitch on the Idle
  staff, in the live view, the review and kept sessions, spoken as words; MusicXML's chord kinds;
  the backing plays a seventh chord as four notes in close position, its top note at or below E4.
  The melody and rhythm guides play as in v3.2.
- **Off reproduces v3.2.** At Off, the same request and seed give exactly v3.2's phrase, in every
  mode, Jam and the rhythm lock included.
- **Storage and replay keys.** The data moves to **data version 5**: the upgrade rewrites player
  presets and kept sessions with the level at Off. Every phrase gets an **`ifk5.`** replay key,
  holding the level by capability name; every `ifk1.` to `ifk4.` key still decodes to the phrase
  the version that wrote it gave.

v3.3 is [Part D](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#part-d--v33) of the v3 master spec.

## How to read the table

The table below is the release gate's evidence
([build plan chunk 22](../../specs/versions/v3/build-plan-v3.3.md#chunk-22--v33-release-gate), plan
v24). It has one row per item of
[master spec §D15](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#d15-definition-of-done), in its order:
items 1 to 27. The plan's
[definition-of-done coverage](../../specs/versions/v3/build-plan-v3.3.md#definition-of-done-coverage)
says which evidence each item needs.

- **Criterion:** the number and a short paraphrase. The spec's wording is the reference.
- **Evidence:** as `file` › 'test title'. A title with `%s`, `%i`, `%j`, `$name` or `${...}` is a
  parameterized test, quoted as written in the source; a test inside a `describe` is named by its
  own title. A hand check or a measurement points to where it is written down. The evidence named
  is a sample of the strongest tests, not every test.
- **Run by:** the npm script that runs the evidence.
- **Status:**
  - **Verified:** automated tests check it, and they pass.
  - **Verified + measured:** a measurement's figure is recorded as well (items 5, 14, 21 and 26).
  - **After the tag:** a hand or listening check owed after the `3.3.0` tag, as a v3.3.x follow-up
    (spec D541); the row's tests are its evidence at the tag.
  - **Skipped by the owner:** L1, the listening check on endings (item 9, spec D540); v3.3 ships
    the triad reading, which the row's tests verify.

The hand checks are written down in
[HARDWARE-CHECKLIST.md, part "v3.3 hand checks"](../../HARDWARE-CHECKLIST.md#v33-hand-checks),
but for v3.3 H2, written there before it runs. The decisions cited as "spec Dn" are in
[specs/decisions.md](../../specs/decisions.md), those cited as "ux Dn" in
[ui_ux/decisions.md](../../ui_ux/decisions.md).
