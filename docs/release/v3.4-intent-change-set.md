was proposed at gate P1 and signed off by the owner with wave 1's report. Applied at once, as the
v3.3 line was once confirmed (chunk 0 of v3.3), rather than waiting for the release sync: VISION
0.12 → 0.13, still STABLE.

- LOGIC, a new paragraph after v3.3's:

  > v3.4, the Lexicon, lets the player look up the music words the app uses: a Lexicon button in
  > the footer opens, between sessions, a page listing every word of the project's lexicon meant
  > for the player, A to Z and by a simple search, each with its plain line first and more on
  > demand, offline, with the keyboard alone and in any orientation. The page shows the project's
  > one lexicon file and nothing else, read when the app is built; it changes, saves and sends

  and after "a staff-only session view;" it adds "word popups and links from other screens to the
  lexicon page; browsing the lexicon by group; an address for one lexicon entry or search;
  searching the lexicon's definitions and app meanings; editing lexicon entries in the app; a
  lexicon backend;".
- TECHNICAL INTERFACE: after "from v3.3, the Seventh chords level, one in session mode and one in
  Jam mode" it adds "; from v3.4, the footer's Lexicon button and the lexicon page".
- EXPECTATION: "the v3.0, v3.1, v3.2 or v3.3 scope above" becomes "the v3.0, v3.1, v3.2, v3.3 or
  v3.4 scope above".
- INTENT: unchanged.

## B. `rule_midi_starting_note` 0.7 → 0.8 and CONTRACT 19.0.0 → 19.0.1 (confirmed 2026-11-23)

`rule_midi_starting_note` (STABLE, BUSINESS, on the contract surface under "Session start") named
only the connection step and the transposition check among what keeps a note from starting a
session. The app also blocks it while My instrument, Your data or a preset sheet is open (before
v3.4), and while the lexicon page is open (from v3.4's wave 1): one flag, reported by the
workspace shell for every sheet and the page, which the starting-note reading checks.

**The owner's confirmation (2026-11-23):** the fuller wording is a clarification of behaviour
already shipped, not a MAJOR change. Applied with the override of the STABLE+BUSINESS guard that
confirmation allows. Kind: **clarifies** (same promise, the cases it already held now named).

- LOGIC: "Nothing played while the player is connecting a controller or running its transposition
  check can start a session, not even that same note, …" → "Nothing played while a sheet is open
  over the workspace (the player connecting a controller or running its transposition check, My
  instrument, Your data or a preset sheet), or while the lexicon page is open, can start a session,
  not even that same note, …"; the rest of the sentence unchanged.
- EXPECTATION, first bullet: "… and only at Idle while a session can start and outside the
  connection step." → "… and only at Idle while a session can start, outside the connection step
  and with no sheet or the lexicon page open."
- EXPECTATION, second bullet: "Every other note, any note played while the review shows, and any
  note played during the connection step or the transposition check, starts nothing …" → "Every
  other note, any note played while the review shows, any note played during the connection step
  or the transposition check, and any note played while My instrument, Your data, a preset sheet or
  the lexicon page is open, starts nothing …".
- INTENT and TECHNICAL INTERFACE: unchanged.

**The bump.** The grid's MAJOR is for a guarantee weakened or removed and its MINOR for an atom
entering the surface (the v3.0 change set's B1 and B2 also took MINOR for a STABLE atom extended).
A clarification of shipped behaviour does neither, so the contract takes a PATCH bump,
`contract_infinite_flow_first_release` 19.0.0 → **19.0.1**, its first. Its LOGIC list is unchanged;
its EXPECTATION gains:

> Version 19.0.1 (PATCH) clarifies a guarantee's wording, with the product owner's explicit
> confirmation, for behaviour the app already has: the starting note, like any note, starts nothing
> while a sheet is open over the workspace (connecting a controller or its transposition check, My
> instrument, Your data or a preset sheet) or while the lexicon page is open. No guarantee is
> weakened, added or removed.
