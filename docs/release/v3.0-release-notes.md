# Infinite Flow v3.0 release notes

Version 3.0.0, not yet tagged. Written on 2026-10-16.

## What v3.0 is

v1 and v2 forget everything on reload. v3.0 is the first release that keeps things on the
player's device, and only there: their settings, their own instrument, the session they just
played and the sessions they chose to keep, and the phrases they marked as favorites. Nothing is
ever sent anywhere. Three smaller items ship with it: generating a few phrases ahead while the app
waits for Start, filtering out the very short "stray" notes some controllers send, and
remembering the controller the player uses. The guiding principle is unchanged: the app
acknowledges and encourages, and never grades or scores the player
([master spec §1 to §3](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#1-core-concept)).

- **Settings:** the generation controls, the theme, the mix, the "During a session" options and
  the review's preferences come back after a reload as the player chose them. "Your data" shows
  what is kept and deletes any of it, or all of it.
- **My instrument:** an instrument the player describes (its range, its transposition, treble or
  bass clef), saved, which the controller's transposition check can set.
- **Kept sessions:** the most recent session kept automatically, pinned sessions kept for good, a
  session list, each kept session reviewable again, and an optional comment per session.
  **Favorites** mark whole phrases, pin their session, and can be exported alone.
- **Played notes:** kept with their session only after the player turns on "Keep my played
  notes", and deletable.
- **The remembered controller:** reselected at load, with no prompt, when the browser already
  allows MIDI access.
- **Pre-generation:** up to four phrases generated at Idle, without ever making Start wait.
- **Stray notes:** very short notes left out of the review's `Played notes` lane and, by default,
  out of the played-notes export.
- **A stale phrase at Start:** when the phrase on screen was made before a change of the settings
  the generation reads, Start and the starting note ask first: Regenerate or Use it.

v3.0 is Part A of the v3 master spec. v3.1, v3.2 and v3.3 follow as their own releases.

## How to read the table

The table below is the release gate's evidence
([build plan chunk 26](../../specs/versions/v3/build-plan.md#chunk-26--v30-release-gate)). It
has one row per item of
[master spec §13](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#13-definition-of-done), in its order:
items 1 to 24.

- **Criterion:** the number and a short paraphrase. The spec's wording is the reference.
- **Evidence:** up to about four tests, six for the long items, as `file` › 'test title'. A title
  with `%s`, `%i`, `$name` or `${...}` is a parameterized test, quoted as written in the source. A
  hand check or a measurement points to where it is written down. The evidence named is a sample
  of the strongest tests, not every test.
- **Run by:** the npm script that runs the evidence.
- **Status:**
  - **Verified:** automated tests check it, and they pass.
  - **Hand check, done:** checked by hand on the date given. Here every one passed on the owner's
    word, 2026-10-16 (spec D340).
  - **Hand check, after the tag (v3.0.x):** may follow the tag; listed in
    [Hand checks after the tag](#hand-checks-after-the-tag-v30x).
  - **Not met:** the criterion does not hold as written.

## Reading notes

- **Item 1** names six of its tests. The other saved settings have their own:
  `tests/e2e/storage/saved-settings.spec.ts` › 'the theme the player chose comes back after a
  reload' and same file › 'Rising tempo turned on comes back stored On: Off and disabled with no
  controller, On once one attaches'. `tests/e2e/storage/played-notes-opt-in.spec.ts` › 'on: the
  played notes are kept and come back after a reload, times from the session start' (item 9) also
  checks that `Keep my played notes` is still on after the reload. The remembered controller is
  item 18's.
- **Item 5.** `optional-scope-absence.spec.ts` supports the row only: the settings are saved
  during it, but the test does not assert that they are.
- **Item 6, "one unpinned earlier included".** No test pins a session, unpins it, then saves a new
  session and checks that it goes. The clause rests on a unit test over every stored unpinned
  session, `tests/unit/components/workspace/workspace-session-save.spec.ts` › 'deletes the summary,
  body and played notes of every readable unpinned kept session, first': its fixtures hold
  `pinned: false`, which is what a later unpin leaves.
- **Item 6, deleting my instrument.** The e2e test covers pinned sessions (one pinned by a
  favorite), favorites and played notes, after a reload. It seeds no comment, and no session has
  just ended at the deletion. The just-ended clause is unit-tested:
  `tests/unit/components/workspace/my-instrument-sessions-batch.spec.ts` (in the row) and
  `tests/unit/components/workspace/workspace-session-controller-my-instrument-sessions.spec.ts` ›
  'counts the session that just ended once, by the key its save minted; leaving closes its Review
  for the list'.
- **Item 12.** The e2e test seeds the settings, my instrument and the remembered controller; no
  kept session or played note exists at the deletion. Coverage holds because Delete everything
  deletes the database itself (`deleteDatabase`), whatever it holds.
- **Item 15, "never delays".** It is shown indirectly: no write happens during a session (item 7),
  so a refusal can only arrive at the flush after the session ends.
- **Item 17, "played notes are still compared at the pitch received".** It rests on a v2
  acknowledgement test; no test plays a note against my instrument and checks the pitch compared.
- **Item 19** has no `release-v3.0` atom: it is governed by `module_phrase_replay_key`, so its row
  cites its tests directly.
- **Item 21, "never affect acknowledgement".** No test is dedicated to it.
  `tests/e2e/session/session-midi-acknowledgement.spec.ts` › 'the note played at its pitch in its
  window takes note-ack, and only it' sends its note through the e2e `tap` helper
  (`tests/e2e/session/session-midi-helpers.ts`), whose note-on and note-off come at the same
  instant, a 0 ms note, and the test acknowledges it.
- **Item 22, "apart from the tests §10 lists".** §10 names "about ten" tests, "among them" its
  examples; it is not a full list. Besides the build plan's §2 rows, 31 v2 test titles changed:
  17 were renamed or moved with the same checks, and 14 check behaviour v3.0 changes on purpose,
  each resting on a section of the master spec (the most recent session kept, §5.1; favorites and
  the favorites-only files keeping their phrase numbers, §6; bass clef for my instrument, §7.1;
  the session seed, §8; `Include very short notes`, §9). The session seed has one effect §10
  does not name: every later phrase's seed now derives from it, so with the same seeds a
  session's phrases after phrase 1 differ from v2's (the export hand-check fixtures were
  regenerated in chunk 23). Every `ifk1.` key still decodes to the same phrase as in v2 (item 19).
- **Item 24, the changes that start at once.** Tested for the tempo (the row's 'with no change, or
  only a tempo change, Start starts at once', and `tests/unit/session/stale-phrase-one.spec.ts` ›
  'never counts the tempo, nor the profile’s customized mark'), a change made and undone (same
  file › 'compares values, not edits: a setting changed and changed back makes nothing stale'), my
  instrument's clef and name (same file › "never counts my instrument's clef or display name") and
  a window crossing (same file › 'never counts the window: a Sixteenth chosen below the
  tablet-landscape width is the chosen Sixteenth'). Rising tempo, Vary settings, Tension across
  phrases, the mix and the theme hold because the stale check compares the generation settings
  only.
