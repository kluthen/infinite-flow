# v3.0 declared-intent change set (pending the owner's confirmation)

Prepared 2026-10-07 by the v3.0 spec ingestion, from the approved v3.0 master spec
([`specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md`](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md)). **Nothing in parts A
to C is applied.** The owner approved the spec; that approval is not the confirmation these parts
need. Each part says what the owner must confirm.

What the ingestion did apply: 37 new BUSINESS atoms at DRAFT, tagged `release-v3.0`, describing
the intended v3.0 behaviour (not built yet), and a `changes-in-v3.0` tag plus a note on every
non-STABLE atom that v3.0 will change (part D). No STABLE atom, the CONTRACT and the VISION were
touched.

## A. CONTRACT 1.8 → 2.0 (MAJOR): three narrowed guarantees

**The owner must confirm, explicitly:** "I confirm the three contract changes: the last-session
review may review other sessions and have favorites; played notes may be stored after I opt in;
the selected controller may be remembered across reloads. Bump the contract to 2.0."

Recommended approach: narrow each STABLE atom to what holds in **both** v2.0 and v3.0, so it stays
STABLE and true of the running code, and let the new v3.0 behaviour live in its DRAFT atoms until
it is built and promoted. Because the narrowed wording still holds for v2.0, the change can be
applied as soon as it is confirmed, and must be applied before the first v3.0 change that breaks
one of the old guarantees merges. The CONTRACT's list of atoms does not change; only their
guarantees narrow.

### A1. `req_last_session_review` 0.4 → 0.5, stays STABLE

- LOGIC: delete "Only the most recently completed session's review exists; no other session can
  be reviewed." Replace "The review has no favorites and no per-phrase action: nothing in it
  edits, cuts or splices generated material." with "Nothing in the review edits, cuts or splices
  generated material."
- EXPECTATION, second bullet: replace "Only the last completed session can be reviewed; the review
  has no favorites and no per-phrase action." with "Nothing in the review edits, cuts or splices
  generated material."
- INTENT, TECHNICAL INTERFACE and every other clause unchanged.
- Guarantee removed: only the last session is reviewable; the review has no favorites. The v3.0
  behaviour lives in `req_kept_session_review`, `req_kept_session_list`, `req_favorite_phrases`.
- Test effect: the "no favorites" check in `tests/e2e/review/review-opens.spec.ts` no longer backs
  this atom; it is replaced by its v3.0 counterpart when favorites are built.

### A2. `rule_optional_midi_privacy` 0.2 → 0.3, stays STABLE

- LOGIC, replace the whole section with: "MIDI input stays private by construction, not by
  policy. Played notes are never sent in any network request, and no export other than the
  played-notes export ever carries them. They are written to browser storage only with the session
  they belong to, and only while the player has opted in to keeping them; without that opt-in they
  exist only in memory, for the current session and its review. The only way a played note ever
  leaves the player's machine is the played-notes export action, which the player explicitly
  triggers."
- TECHNICAL INTERFACE: "No network path carries played notes; the only storage path for them is
  their kept session, used only after the player's opt-in; the played-notes export is a local file
  download the player triggers."
- EXPECTATION: "Under a network and storage spy across a full session, its review and every
  export, no played note is sent over the network; without the player's opt-in to keeping them, no
  played note is written to storage; and played notes leave the page only in a played-notes export
  file the player triggered."
- Guarantee removed: played notes are never written to browser storage. The v3.0 behaviour lives
  in `rule_played_notes_kept_only_after_opt_in` and `rule_kept_played_notes_deletable`.
- Test effect: the storage half of the review privacy spy becomes "only allowed data, played notes
  only after opt-in"; its network half stays.

### A3. `rule_same_controller_reattaches` 0.1 → 0.2, stays STABLE

- LOGIC: delete the last sentence, "The kept selection lives in memory only and does not survive a
  page reload: after a reload the player goes through the connection step again."
- TECHNICAL INTERFACE: "The selected input is remembered for the page's lifetime and reattached
  when the same device reappears." (drops "in memory").
- EXPECTATION, third bullet: "A different device is not picked up until the player goes through the
  connection step." (drops "after a page reload no selection is remembered").
- Guarantee removed: the selection is forgotten on reload. The v3.0 behaviour lives in
  `rule_last_controller_remembered`.
- Test effect: the reload case in `tests/e2e/controller/controller-status.spec.ts` no longer backs
  this atom; it is replaced by the v3.0 "controller remembered" test.

### A4. `contract_infinite_flow_first_release` 1.8 → 2.0

- LOGIC and TECHNICAL INTERFACE unchanged (same atoms on the surface).
- EXPECTATION: append "Version 2.0 (MAJOR) narrows three guarantees for v3.0, with the product
  owner's explicit confirmation: the last-session review no longer guarantees that only the last
  session can be reviewed or that the review has no favorites; played notes may be written to
  storage with their session once the player opts in, and are still never sent; and the selected
  controller is no longer guaranteed to be forgotten on reload. Every other guarantee is
  unchanged."
- notes: record the date and the owner's confirmation.

## B. Two STABLE atoms to extend when v3.0 is built (no guarantee weakened)

**The owner must confirm** each revised text when its code lands; each then takes a MINOR
CONTRACT bump. Applying them now would claim, at STABLE, behaviour that is not built.

- `rule_instrument_profile_range`: it names the three built-in profiles' ranges and windows. Add
  that a player-made instrument obeys the same rule, with whole-number endpoints, a span of at least
  24 semitones, and register windows derived by the same method. Until then the DRAFT
  `rule_my_instrument_range_limits` holds this.
- `rule_controller_transposition_check`: it suggests a device setting or a better-matching
  profile. Add the third outcome, "use this for my instrument". Until then the DRAFT
  `rule_transposition_check_sets_my_instrument` holds this.

No change needed: `rule_review_played_notes_toggle` (filtering very short notes from the lane, and
saving the switch, weaken none of its guarantees); `rule_mutation_reverts_at_session_end` and
`rule_adaptive_tempo_resets_after_session` (saving keeps the player's choice, which they already
restore).

## C. VISION 0.7 → 0.8, and retiring the chord-fit atoms

**The owner must confirm** the vision text below, and the retirement of the four chord-fit atoms.
Until then the 37 v3.0 atoms sit outside the vision's scope.

- LOGIC, add after the v2 paragraph (and its v2.4 bullet): "Version 3 begins with v3.0, Storage,
  which keeps things on the player's device, and only there: their settings and preferences; one
  instrument they describe themselves, My instrument, which the controller-transposition check can
  set; the controller they last chose; the most recent session, kept automatically, and the
  sessions they pin, each reviewable again and each with an optional comment; favorite phrases,
  which pin their session, with an export of favorites only; and the notes they played, kept only
  after an explicit opt-in. The player sees what is kept, and deletes any of it or all of it, in
  one place. v3.0 also generates up to four phrases ahead while the app waits at Idle, without
  ever making Start wait, and leaves very short stray controller notes out of the review and, by
  default, out of the played-notes export. Nothing is ever sent anywhere: data leaves the device
  only through an export the player triggers."
- LOGIC, replace the "Out of scope through v2" sentence with: "Out of scope through v3.0:
  generation seeded from anything the player played; embellishment flags and player-made presets;
  a key and mode suggestion; a rhythm lock; rhythm ratios and a rhythm editor; a backup file of
  everything kept, to move it to another browser; learning the player's taste from how they play;
  marking a range of measures as a favorite; remembering a controller's latency, or a player-facing
  latency calibration step; editing, cutting or splicing generated material; richer review tooling
  such as passage replay or expected-against-played views; a quiet per-measure acknowledgement of
  off-script playing that fits the harmony; accompaniment with musical roles (harmonizing,
  responding, contrasting); playback of the generated lead line; a staff-only session view;
  microphone capture or acoustic pitch detection; accounts, sharing or server-side state; browsers
  other than Chromium-based ones." The "Permanently out of scope" sentence stays.
- TECHNICAL INTERFACE, append: "From v3.0 it adds a place that lists and deletes what is kept, a
  list of kept sessions with their reviews, favorites and comments, a switch for keeping played
  notes, and My instrument."
- EXPECTATION, replace with: "Scope decisions keep the product a non-evaluative, browser-only
  practice partner. Every business atom falls within the first release, the v2 scope (release
  v2.0, with its parts v2.1, v2.2 and v2.4) or the v3.0 scope above; nothing listed as out of
  scope enters a release without this scope being changed first; nothing ever grades, scores,
  ranks or marks the player wrong."
- tags: add `v3`.
- Retire, in the same change: `req_chord_fit_acknowledgement`, `rule_chord_fit_criteria`,
  `rule_chord_fit_measure_assignment`, `rule_chord_fit_only_off_script`. The v3 scope drops the
  end-of-measure chord-fit check; no code or test links them; the revised expectation no longer
  carves out v3 nice-to-have atoms. Each now carries a note saying so.
