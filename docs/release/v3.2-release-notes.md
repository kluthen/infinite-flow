## What v3.2 is

Until v3.2 the app only ever continues its own ideas. v3.2 lets the player give it one: a short
idea played on the controller, which the app then takes further
([master spec C1](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#c1-core-concept)). The guiding principle is
unchanged: the app acknowledges and encourages, and never grades or scores the player.

- **Two modes.** The app always opens in **session mode**: the app as v3.1 ships it, plus v3.2's
  session settings. A button at Idle switches to **Jam mode**, a mode with its own small set of
  settings, and back. Jam's code is loaded only on the first press of that button, so it stays out
  of the first load.
- **Jam.** After a one-measure count-in, the player plays a **doodle** (a short idea of one to four
  measures) over the app's click. The app reads it as written notes on a sixteenth-note grid, then
  writes phrase 1, which continues from the doodle's last note, shows it for one read-in measure
  and plays on with rolling phrases, as any session does. The doodle sets Jam's rhythm and shape
  controls (note density, rhythmic variety, syncopation, silence density, contour, leapiness,
  register), and steers which of the phrases those controls allow come first: a rhythm close to the
  doodle's, the same ups and downs. **Drift** (Close, Near, Free) says how far the phrases may
  wander from it. The doodle never changes the key or mode. Jam needs a MIDI controller.
- **The kept doodle.** After a Jam, Jam mode keeps its doodle, with phrase 1 already written from
  it: the next Jam plays on the same doodle. `New doodle` lets it go; a kept Jam session's review
  offers `Jam again on this doodle`.
- **Tempo and tap-in.** Jam's tempo runs 60 to 180 BPM in steps of 10. `Tap tempo` sets it from
  four taps, on the button or on the controller.
- **The key and mode suggestion.** After a Jam, the review may say which key and mode the doodle
  sounds in, worked out from its notes, and offer to use it for the next Jams. "Key" here names the
  **home note** (the note a scale feels at rest on, also called the tonic), and "mode" the scale
  built on it (Ionian, Dorian, ... Locrian; Major is Ionian and Minor is Aeolian). A doodle using
  notes none of the app's modes holds gets one plain sentence instead, naming no "wrong" note.
- **The rhythm lock** (session mode; Jam has its own). Every phrase repeats phrase 1's rhythm while
  the pitches keep changing, so the eyes can go to the notes. **Allowed rhythm changes** lets a
  phrase now and then change that rhythm in one small way (move a note, move a rest, split a note,
  merge two notes, turn a note into a rest or back), the next phrase going back to it.
- **What may vary.** Vary settings' six groups (Key and mode, Harmony, Tension, Rhythm, Melody
  shape, Figures) say which settings it may move.
- **The guide playback** (both modes; added after approval, spec D490). Two optional sounds play
  the phrase on the sheet while it is played: the **melody guide** its notes, the **rhythm guide**
  its rhythm on the chords' roots (the **root** of a chord is the note it is built on and named
  after). Both are off by default; they change only what is heard.
- **Session mode unchanged by default.** With the lock off and all six groups on, session mode
  generates exactly what v3.1 generates, and a request with none of v3.2's inputs gives v3.1's
  phrase byte for byte.

v3.2 is Part C of the v3 master spec.

## How to read the table

The table below is the release gate's evidence
([build plan chunk 27](../../specs/versions/v3/build-plan-v3.2.md#chunk-27--v32-release-gate),
plan v41). It has one row per item of
[master spec C16](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#c16-definition-of-done), in its order:
items 1 to 52. The plan's
[definition-of-done coverage](../../specs/versions/v3/build-plan-v3.2.md#definition-of-done-coverage)
says which evidence each item needs.

- **Criterion:** the number and a short paraphrase. The spec's wording is the reference.
- **Evidence:** as `file` › 'test title'. A title with `%s`, `%i`, `$name` or `${...}` is a
  parameterized test, quoted as written in the source; a test inside a `describe` is named by its
  own title. A hand check or a measurement points to where it is written down. The evidence named
  is a sample of the strongest tests, not every test.
- **Run by:** the npm script that runs the evidence.
- **Status:**
  - **Verified:** automated tests check it, and they pass.
  - **Verified + measured:** a measurement's figure is recorded as well (items 1, 2, 10, 12, 16
    and 31).
  - **Hand check, done:** run on the owner's word, on the date given.
  - **After the tag:** a hand or listening check owed after the `3.2.0` tag, as a v3.2.x follow-up
    (spec D521); the row's tests are its evidence at the tag.
  - **Not met:** the criterion does not hold as written.

The hand checks are written down in
[HARDWARE-CHECKLIST.md, part "v3.2 hand checks"](../../HARDWARE-CHECKLIST.md#v32-hand-checks). The
decisions cited as "spec Dn" are in [specs/decisions.md](../../specs/decisions.md).

## Reading notes

- **Item 3, "review, exports".** No test opens the review or an export of a locked session and
  looks for a mark. The clause holds by construction: the kept record holds no lock outcome and no
  rhythm change (`kept-session-v32-codec.spec.ts` › "keeps the lock's outcome and the rhythm change
  out of every record"), and the review and the exports are built from the kept record and the
  phrases' timelines, whose export goldens are unchanged since 3.1.0.
- **Item 8, "presets never hold or change them".** No test chooses a preset and then checks the
  three settings. A preset holds its ten controls, seven levels, restriction and kind, and nothing
  else (`preset-selection.spec.ts` › 'carries over no other setting: no tempo, key, mode or
  instrument, no extra field'). **"v3.0 settings load with the defaults"**: v3.0's settings reach
  the three by the same path as v3.1's, a store holding none of their records, which the v3.1 tests
  cover.
- **Item 11, "the replay tool decodes all four".** The replay script's own spec runs `ifk1.`,
  `ifk2.` and `ifk4.` keys; `ifk3.` keys go through the same decoder and replay
  (`replayDecodedPhraseKey`) in the v3.1 corpus test, not through the script.
- **Item 16, "both work offline".** The offline test opens Jam mode; no test opens a kept Jam
  session's review offline. The review's doodle parts are in the same `jam-mode` chunk the service
  worker keeps.
- **Item 24, "never feed the suggestion".** The suggestion reads the quantized doodle, from which
  the quantizer has already left out every very short note (`doodle-quantizer.spec.ts`'s three
  "never" tests); no suggestion test plays one.
- **Item 33, "Delete everything lets it go"**, and **item 47**. Delete everything reloads the page
  (`your-data.spec.ts`), and a reload keeps no doodle in memory
  (`kept-doodle-lets-go.spec.ts` › 'a reload keeps nothing; …'); no test runs Delete everything with
  a doodle kept. Session mode's three new settings are records in the settings store Delete
  everything empties, so they load as their defaults after it.
- **Item 35, "nothing asked or offered".** The suggestion is worked out once, at the doodle's end,
  and shown only by the review's row and the kept doodle's bar; the bar is held still, every button
  disabled, during a Jam on the doodle. No end-to-end test watches a running Jam for an offer.
- **Item 43, "never during a Jam"**: as item 35.
- **Item 44, "a crossing to the modes only on confirmation".** The answer is named in the player's
  family (`key-suggestion-names.spec.ts`), and Use it goes through the same change as a change by
  hand at Jam's Idle (`use-jam-settings.spec.ts`); no test makes Use it cross from Major or Minor to
  the modes and checks the confirmation.
