and H5 after the release (spec D397, replacing D392).

## What v3.1 is

Until v3.1 the player chooses between two fixed styles, Neutral and Jazz, and has no say over how
the melody is decorated. v3.1 lets the player choose which decorations the app uses, and save a
whole set of generation settings under a name
([master spec B1](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#b1-core-concept)). The guiding principle
is unchanged: the app acknowledges and encourages, and never grades or scores the player.

- **Embellishment figures, each with a level:** an embellishment (also called a decoration) is a
  note, or a small group of notes, that decorates a more important note without changing the
  chord underneath. Seven kinds, called figures (passing tone, accented passing tone, neighbor
  tone, appoggiatura, diatonic enclosure, chromatic approach, chromatic enclosure), each get a
  level in the new Embellishments group. The player sees `Off`, `Rarely`, `Often` and
  `Very often`; the spec calls the same four levels Off, As today, Favored and Strongly favored. A
  higher level makes the app try that figure earlier, so it is heard more often. A level is a
  preference, never a quota: no phrase is refused for lacking a figure. The free appoggiatura and
  the diatonic enclosure are new.
- **Presets:** a preset is a named set of generation settings chosen in one step: ten controls
  and the seven levels. Eight built-in presets ship (Neutral, Jazz, Gentle, Speed drill, Reel,
  Bebop, Pop song, Modal (Dorian)), and the player can save, rename and delete their own, the
  player presets, up to 50.
- **The key and mode restriction, and the highlight:** a preset may be meant for some keys and
  modes only (Reel: Mixolydian or Dorian). Choosing it moves the key and mode inside its
  restriction; the key and mode controls then mark what it allows. Nothing is ever blocked.
- **Custom names its base:** changing a setting the selected preset holds turns the selection into
  "Custom, based on" that preset; only choosing a preset leaves Custom.
- **Saved on the device:** the levels, the player presets and the selection come back after a
  reload. v3.0's saved settings and kept sessions carry over: a v3.0 Jazz selection with a changed
  setting comes back as "Custom, based on Jazz".
- **The `ifk3.` replay key:** every phrase's key now holds the seven levels, not a style name;
  `ifk1.` and `ifk2.` keys still replay to the phrase their version wrote.
- **Neutral and Jazz unchanged:** at their own levels they generate exactly what v3.0 generates.

v3.1 is Part B of the v3 master spec.

## How to read the table

The table below is the release gate's evidence
([build plan chunk 21](../../specs/versions/v3/build-plan-v3.1.md#chunk-21--v31-release-gate)).
It has one row per item of
[master spec B14](../../specs/versions/v3/master-spec.md#b14-definition-of-done), in its order:
items 1 to 23.

- **Criterion:** the number and a short paraphrase. The spec's wording is the reference.
- **Evidence:** up to about five tests, six for the long items, as `file` › 'test title'. A title
  with `%s`, `%i`, `$name` or `${...}` is a parameterized test, quoted as written in the source. A
  hand check or a measurement points to where it is written down. The evidence named is a sample
  of the strongest tests, not every test.
- **Run by:** the npm script that runs the evidence.
- **Status:**
  - **Verified:** automated tests check it, and they pass.
  - **Verified + measured:** a measurement's acceptance is met as well (items 3 and 8, and item
    9's sweep half).
  - **Hand check, done:** v3.1 H1 and H2, passed on the owner's word on 2026-10-21 (spec D391).
  - **Hand check, open:** v3.1 H3, H4 and H5, run by the owner after the release (spec D397).
  - **Not met:** the criterion does not hold as written.

The hand checks are written down in
[HARDWARE-CHECKLIST.md, part "v3.1 hand checks"](../../HARDWARE-CHECKLIST.md#v31-hand-checks).

## Reading notes

- **Item 10, "never changes the session options".** No test chooses a preset and then checks the
  session options. The clause holds by construction: a preset holds its id, name, ten controls,
  seven levels, restriction and kind, and nothing else
  (`tests/unit/domain/presets/preset-selection.spec.ts` › 'carries over no other setting: no
  tempo, key, mode or instrument, no extra field').
- **Item 19, "no restriction".** `phrase-replay-key-ifk3.spec.ts` › 'holds no musical profile or
  preset name and no customized flag' does not name the restriction. The key's payload is exactly
  seven entries in a stated order (same file › 'holds exactly seven entries in the stated order,
  null at index 6 for a built-in'), none of them a restriction.
- **Item 20, "unchanged by a later deletion".** The rename test shows the label is stored as text
  with the kept session (`presetLabel`) and not read back from the preset; no test deletes the
  preset and then reads a kept session's label.
