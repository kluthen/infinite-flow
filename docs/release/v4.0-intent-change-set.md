**Words used below.** The **lead** is the player's line, the phrase on the staff. A **system
voice** is any line the app plays. A **part** is one of v4.0's four new system voices, named here
by its job, its **accompaniment role**, rather than by its working label (Thirds, Counter-melody,
Fills, Drums), so a later label change needs no rewording: the **highlighting part** (parallel
thirds and sixths below the lead), the **contrasting part** (a slow counter-melody), the
**responding part** (fills in the lead's rests) and the **drum part**. The **relation parts** are
the first three. The **sound library** is the software-synthesis library the parts and the
backing play on. Music terms are in the [lexicon](../music/lexicon.md).

### A1. `req_session_backing_track` 0.3 → 0.4: meaning changes

- In plain words, today: the backing is made from simple oscillators, with no sample library and no
  extra download, and the atom says there is no accompaniment with musical roles. Proposed: the
  backing plays the same notes on the sound library's software voices, still with no recorded
  sound, and falls back to its oscillator voices when the library is not ready at Start or failed
  to load; the accompaniment parts exist beside it.
- LOGIC, third bullet: "It is synthesized in the app itself from simple oscillators, with no sample
  library and no extra download, so it stays usable offline and carries no licensing
  entanglement." becomes:

  > It is synthesized in the app itself, with no recorded sound: the bass and the chords play on the
  > software-synthesized voices of the app's sound library, which is part of the app and kept for
  > offline use, and on the app's own oscillator voices for a session started before the library
  > was ready, or after it failed to load. It stays usable offline and carries no licensing
  > entanglement.

- LOGIC, fifth bullet: "There is no playback of the generated lead line, and no accompaniment with
  musical roles such as harmonizing, responding to or contrasting with the player." is removed.
  Its first half has been untrue since v3.2's melody guide; its second half since v4.0's parts.
- EXPECTATION, last bullet: unchanged ("requires no additional audio assets" still holds).
- INTENT, TECHNICAL INTERFACE, the bass and the chords' notes: unchanged.

### A2. `req_session_audio_mix` 0.2 → 0.3: extends

- INTENT: "Let the player balance the backing, the click and each guide at any moment." becomes
  "Let the player balance the backing, the click, each guide and each accompaniment part at any
  moment."
- LOGIC becomes:

  > The mix's controls are an on/off switch and a volume for each of the backing, the click, the
  > melody guide, the rhythm guide and the four accompaniment parts: sixteen separate controls,
  > each independent of the others, so every combination of the backing, the two guides and the
  > four parts is valid, reachable at all times, at Idle, in the review and throughout a session,
  > and shared by both modes, except that the four parts' switches are disabled while the sound
  > library has failed to load. They are not generation settings and change nothing about what is
  > generated. Turning the backing, a guide or a part off only silences its sound; it never affects
  > timing.

- TECHNICAL INTERFACE: "Eight independent audio-mix controls, … the melody guide and the rhythm
  guide, stay operable …" becomes "Sixteen independent audio-mix controls, an on/off switch and a
  volume for each of the backing, the click, the melody guide, the rhythm guide and the four
  accompaniment parts, stay operable at Idle, in the review and during a session, in both modes,
  the parts' switches aside while the sound library has failed to load."
- EXPECTATION, first bullet: "Each of the eight controls acts independently at any moment, during
  a session or not." becomes "Each of the sixteen controls acts independently at any moment, during
  a session or not, the parts' switches aside while the sound library has failed to load."

## B. The CONTRACT: no bump now

This ingestion touches no STABLE atom, so CONTRACT stays at 19.1.0. Recommendation:

- **Bump at the wave syncs, not now and not only at release.** Each wave sync that applies an A
  item takes one MAJOR bump for all the A items it applies (the first one 20.0.0), as v3.3 did
  (D517). Bumping now would announce guarantees the code does not keep; bumping only at the
  release would leave STABLE atoms contradicting merged code between waves.
- **Promotions at the release sync**, each with a MINOR bump and the owner's agreement: the
  `release-v4.0` atoms that qualify for the surface, once built, linked and checked.
- **One early candidate:** `rule_no_recorded_sound` states the owner's only true sound ban (D553),
  which the shipped app already keeps. Recommended for STABLE at the sync that applies A1, linked
  to the build's recorded-sound check, so the guarantee A1 drops from the backing ("no sample
  library") stays on the surface in the same MAJOR bump, as its own rule.

## C. VISION v4.0 line (proposed, for the owner's sign-off)

Until it is signed off, every `release-v4.0` atom falls outside VISION 0.13, which lists
"accompaniment with musical roles (harmonizing, responding, contrasting)" as out of scope.
Proposed: VISION 0.13 → 0.14, still STABLE.

- LOGIC, a new paragraph after v3.4's:

  > v4.0, Accompaniment with roles, lets the player hear, on demand, up to four accompaniment parts
  > the app plays beside the lead, each doing one job for it: a highlighting part doubling the
  > lead's important notes a third or a sixth below, a contrasting part playing a slow
  > counter-melody on the chords' guide tones, a responding part answering the lead with short
  > fills in its rests, and a drum part keeping one simple groove. The player still plays only the
  > lead. Each part has its own switch, volume and playing light and is off by default; it is
  > rendered from the written phrase alone, heard only, never drawn on the score, and compares
  > nothing with the player's playing. The generated-notes MIDI file can carry, beside the backing,
  > each part that was on at the session's end as its own track. Every voice the app plays, the
  > backing's included, is software synthesis with a sound the app chooses; the one ban on sound is
  > on recorded sound: samples and audio files.

- LOGIC, the out-of-scope list: "Out of scope through v3.4" becomes "Out of scope through v4.0";
  "accompaniment with musical roles (harmonizing, responding, contrasting);" is removed; after
  "playback of the generated lead line outside a running session, or with a sound the player
  chooses;" it adds "choosing an instrument or a sound for any voice; drawing an accompaniment part
  on the score; accompaniment that answers what the player actually played; more accompaniment
  techniques (echo or imitation, comping, a walking bass), drum fills, grooves per style, swing and
  style packs; fills or bass approaches that lead into the next phrase;".
- TECHNICAL INTERFACE: after "from v3.4, the footer's Lexicon button and the lexicon page" it adds
  "; from v4.0, the four accompaniment parts' switches, volumes and playing lights in the audio
  mix, and the generated-notes MIDI file's accompaniment option".
- EXPECTATION: "the v3.0, v3.1, v3.2, v3.3 or v3.4 scope above" becomes "the v3.0, v3.1, v3.2, v3.3,
  v3.4 or v4.0 scope above".
- INTENT: unchanged. Tags: `v4` added.
