# Neutral and Jazz Apply Their Defined Settings

## INTENT
Make the Neutral and Jazz presets establish their defined settings.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
The Neutral preset is the default and selects gentle diatonic harmonic motion, Lift tension, quarter-note density, Slightly varied rhythmic variety, straight syncopation, sparse rests, arch contour, middle register, Shaped leapiness and Varied arpeggio chaos. The Jazz preset selects gentle diatonic harmonic motion, Lift tension, eighth-note density, Slightly varied rhythmic variety, occasional offbeats, conversational space, wandering contour, varying register, Shaped leapiness and Varied arpeggio chaos. Each also holds its seven figure levels: the passing tone, the accented passing tone and the neighbor tone As today in both; the appoggiatura and the diatonic enclosure Off in both; the chromatic approach and the chromatic enclosure Off in Neutral and As today in Jazz. Selecting either preset replaces all ten preset-held controls and all seven figure levels with that preset's complete defined bundle, regardless of the previous configuration, Tension, Leapiness and Arpeggio chaos included.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
The player selects the Neutral or Jazz preset.

## EXPECTATION
Selecting either preset replaces every one of the ten preset-held controls and the seven figure levels with its specified bundle. Selecting Jazz or Neutral applies gentle diatonic harmonic motion, Lift tension, Slightly varied rhythmic variety, Shaped leapiness and Varied arpeggio chaos; from a configuration with Tension at Build, selecting Neutral sets Lift.
