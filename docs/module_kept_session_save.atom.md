# Session Save and Most Recent Session

## INTENT
Save the session that just ended once, as its review opens, so that it and the pinned sessions are what is kept; while the visit saves, delete every kept session found damaged, at load, at that save or when it is opened, and tell how many went; and say which session is the most recent one, both within the visit and after a reload.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
When. The session's save is asked for at a session's end that opens a review, that is, after at least one phrase sounded, right after the review opens and never waited for, so the end is not delayed; it runs once the release's flush is over, and a session started before it runs makes it wait for that session's end. A session ended during its count-in has no review: its end reads, saves and deletes nothing, and the most recent session does not change.

Order. At an end with a review, three steps run in this order. First, the settings flush: the release of the save policy's session hold, which writes the settings changed during the session in a transaction of its own, and opens no transaction when no setting changed. Second, the keyed read of the session-wide records of the kept sessions, each with its key, read at the save rather than taken from the load, so that a session another tab saved meanwhile is counted. Third, one batch, applied in one transaction, all or none, that writes the new session under the key the save mints for it (its session-wide record and its body, and its played notes only when Keep my played notes is on at the session's end and the session holds a played note in a phrase) and deletes the session-wide record, the body and the played notes of every readable kept session that is not pinned and of every kept session whose session-wide record is damaged, the deletions listed before the writes so that a session kept under the very same key is replaced. The settings and the session are kept in separate transactions so that a storage-full refusal of the session never loses the player's setting changes, and so that a refusal is known to be the session's own or a setting's.

Readable, damaged and newer kept sessions. A kept session is readable when its session-wide record decodes as a current record of this version. It is damaged when the kept-session codec reads it as unreadable: at the save and at load, from its session-wide record alone; at an opening, from all its records, any of them unreadable, refused in content, missing (the body, or the played notes the session-wide record says are kept) or disagreeing with another. A session-wide record a newer version wrote is never damaged: it is left in place, with its body and played notes, and never deleted. A read the device refuses, and a session whose records are all absent (deleted meanwhile), are not damage and delete nothing. A keyed read that is refused at the save deletes nothing, and the new session is still saved in a batch with no deletions.

Damaged kept sessions deleted. While saving is on, a damaged kept session is deleted, its session-wide record, its body and its played notes in one batch, pinned or not, at three moments, and never while saving is off. At the session's save, the save's own batch deletes every kept session whose session-wide record the keyed read finds damaged, in the same transaction as the new session's writes, and the save tells how many damaged sessions it deleted. At load, a clean-up runs in the session controller's chunk as soon as the controller is built, never in the entry chunk, and only when a summary the load read is damaged, so a visit with nothing damaged opens no transaction for it: one batch through the save policy's kept-change line (changeKept, its refusal resolved to its caller alone, never to the failure listener), never through the plain save, which during a session would hold the change and send a later refusal to the failure listener as a failed save. The clean-up reads the summaries again at the change and deletes every kept session whose summary it then finds damaged: a summary damaged since the load goes too, its row with it, and when none is damaged at that read it writes nothing and tells nothing. Queued like every kept change, it runs after the session saves asked for before it and waits for a running session's end. The list's rows and the cue row's links are decided without those sessions, as they already are. When the re-read deletes the most recent session while it is a kept session not yet read into memory, its summary damaged since the load, the most recent session becomes at once the readable kept session that started last among the summaries the re-read found readable, or there is none and review-link goes. A most recent session held in memory (one that ended in this visit, or one opened from its row) stays the most recent, readable in memory; its records are among those deleted only if another tab damaged them after this tab read or wrote them, and then its row goes with the deletion and the deletion's count includes it. At an opening (Review last session after a reload, or a list row), the read of a kept session for its review gives the session, or tells that it is damaged, or that it was not read (the device refused the read, its records are all absent, or a newer version wrote it), instead of giving nothing for all three. A damaged one is deleted at once, in one batch through the same kept-change line, the opening staying under way until that batch settles; the batch reads the session's records again at the change and deletes them only while they still read as damaged, writing nothing otherwise; its row then leaves, and when it was the most recent session, the most recent session becomes at once the readable kept session that started last among the rows held, as the load would have chosen, unlike the player's own deletion, which leaves none for the visit. A session not read keeps the failed-to-open path (module_kept_session_reach). While saving is off the load runs no clean-up, and a damaged session found at an opening takes the failed-to-open path; so does one whose deletion resolves saving off or finds it no longer damaged. A deletion of damaged sessions the device refuses changes nothing stored: the sessions it named stay unlisted for the visit and are counted nowhere, an opening lands as after the deletion, and the next load tries again.

What is told. Every committed deletion of damaged sessions, at load, at the session's save (its retry included) or at an opening, is told with how many sessions it deleted; a refused one with its refusal's kind and how many sessions it named. The reports reach the notice row's notices through a channel built with the workspace, before the session controller's chunk loads: it is handed to the kept-sessions seam, through which the session controller tells both, and the app's root component passes it to the workspace shell, which hands it to the notices, as the remembered controller's store reaches the shell. A committed deletion gives the apology; a refusal for lack of space gives Storage full, and any other refusal the save-problem notice's Damaged session not deleted wording. The notices take both refusals from the deletion's report, never from the save policy's failure listener, the refusal being resolved to its caller alone. A refused session save tells no deletion of damaged sessions, its keep line telling the refusal as for any failed session save; an abandoned save, an end while saving is off and a refused read tell nothing.

The session's own save, marked by its result. The save policy's session save resolves that save's own outcome to its caller: saved; refused, with the refusal's kind; saving off, saving being off for the visit or the batch refused as outdated; or abandoned, the page going away (below). This outcome is separate from the save policy's one failure listener, which drives the notice row for every other save. While the save runs, the session is the most recent one and its keep line reads "Not pinned: deleted when your next session is saved." Saved: the session stays so, not pinned. Refused: nothing is written or deleted, the session is held in memory as the most recent one, every control of its review works in memory, and its keep line reads "Not saved on this device: export it to keep it."; a refusal for lack of space also raises the storage-full notice, and a refusal for any other reason raises no notice, the Save failed notice being for saves other than the session's own.

Saving off. While saving is off for the visit (an outdated tab, or a visit started from the defaults because the saved data could not be read), the end reads, saves and deletes nothing: the session is held in memory as after a refused save, its keep line reads "Not saved on this device: export it to keep it." at once, known from the saving state rather than from a save that may never settle, and every kept session stays. Below the tablet-landscape width the keep line shows only in its "Not saved" form.

The page going away. A session cut short by the page going away (closed, reloaded, or left for another address) is never saved. The save takes a watch on the page's leaving as it starts, before the codec loads: the watch reads set at once if the page is going away then, or once the page's leaving fires after it, and then stays set, even when the browser brings the page back from its back-forward cache. The save checks it once the codec has loaded and hands it to the save policy, which checks it before the keyed read and before the batch; once it is set the save stops there, with nothing more read or written, and resolves abandoned. An abandoned save is settled as not saved: the session is held in memory as the most recent one, its keep line reads "Not saved on this device: export it to keep it.", every kept session stays, no notice shows and no row is added; unlike a refused save it is never resumed or retried. On a reload or close the page's leaving comes before the page turns hidden, in the same task, so the save of the session that the hidden page ends never starts its first transaction; a save still running when the page leaves stops at its next step. A tab or app switch is no leaving: its session ends, opens its review and is saved. A page brought back from the back-forward cache is no longer going away: a session that ends after the return, and the retry of a refused save run after it, take a new watch and save as usual.

The retry. A session whose own save was refused is not changed through the path kept sessions take after their save (module_kept_session_changes). Its next favorite mark, pin, unpin or comment runs the session's own save again instead: the same batch, under the same key, with its pinned fact, its favorites and its comment as held in memory then, its played notes kept as decided at its end (turning Keep my played notes on afterwards adds none), and the unpinned kept sessions to delete read at the retry. A change made while a retry runs waits for it. When the retry succeeds, the keep line takes its pinned or not-pinned wording; a failed retry changes nothing shown. A retry stops before its transaction, writing nothing, once its session has left memory. While a batch that erases my instrument's kept sessions is applied (entity_my_instrument_record), a retry of a counted session's save is held back before its transaction; once that batch is saved, the session leaves memory and the retry goes with it; when it is refused or resolves saving off, a retry it held back runs again, once any retry under way is over, while the session is still held, not saved, and saving is on.

The most recent session. Within the visit, it is the last session that ended with a review, saved or held in memory. After a reload, it is the readable kept session with the latest start, known from the summaries the load read; Review last session reads its records by its key, showing the read as under way, and once read it is held in memory and its review shows, unless a session started or the most recent session changed meanwhile. A read that does not give the session (refused, its records all absent, or written by a newer version) shows no review, and what the screen shows instead is module_kept_session_reach's; damaged records are deleted as above, the list showing in the review's place without that session, and the most recent session becomes the readable kept session that started last, or none. It stays in memory across the next Start, which hides its review but does not clear it, until the next session ends with a review and takes its place; a session ended during its count-in leaves it in place.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
The end in src/components/workspace/workspace-session-ends.ts opens the review over endedSession(history, keeping) (src/components/workspace/session-review-state.ts), makes it the most recent session and calls saveEndedSession(saves, page, history, saving) (src/components/workspace/workspace-session-save.ts), its saves the visit's saves wrapped by the session list's rows (savingThrough, src/components/workspace/use-kept-sessions.ts; module_kept_session_reach) and its page the session browser ports' PageLeaving (src/session/session-browser-ports.ts), whose watch() gives a check that reads true once the page is going away and stays so; its browser adapter, createWindowPageLeaving(target) and createBrowserPageLeaving() in src/components/workspace/browser-page-visibility.ts, sets the page as going away at each pagehide and clears it at a pageshow whose persisted is true, whose outcome settledKeeping applies to the most recent session and to the review while it draws that session. Its saving argument is endedSessionSave(history, keepPlayedNotes): the key minted once, and whether the session's played notes are kept. A fifth argument, held, the HeldSessionFacts (pinned, favorites, comment) the save records, is none at the end (ENDED_SESSION_FACTS) and the facts held in memory at a retry; the end tells a refused save to createKeptSessionEdits (src/components/workspace/workspace-kept-session-edits.ts) through saveRefused(key, history), which retries it. saveEndedSession loads the kept-session codec on demand and calls the visit saver's saveSession (SessionSaveHold in src/storage/settings-saver.ts, queued behind release()), which reaches SavePolicy.saveSession(changes, pageGoingAway) (module_save_policy), handing it that check, resolving a SessionSaveOutcome ({ status: 'saved' }, { status: 'refused', kind }, { status: 'saving-off' } or { status: 'abandoned' }; saveEndedSession itself resolves abandoned when the check reads true once the codec has loaded). saveEndedSession resolves it as an EndedSessionOutcome, a saved one also giving damagedDeleted, how many damaged kept sessions its batch deleted, which tellDamagedDeleted(reports, outcome) tells through the seam's reports when it is above zero, called by the end (workspace-session-ends.ts) and by the retry (workspace-kept-session-edits.ts). How a session is kept is a SessionKeeping: 'pinned', 'not-pinned' or 'not-saved'. The most recent session is the controller state's mostRecent, a MostRecentSession ({ kind: 'held', session } or { kind: 'kept', key }) that Start does not clear, with readingMostRecent while it is read (src/components/workspace/workspace-session-controller-types.ts); src/components/workspace/workspace-session-start.ts is not changed for it. ## EXPECTATION
- At an end with a review, a setting changed during the session is flushed in a transaction before the session's batch; with no setting changed, the only transaction is the session's batch.
- After a second session's save, one unpinned kept session remains, under a key of the form `session-{startedAt}-{sessionSeed}`; every pinned kept session remains.
- A kept session whose session-wide record is damaged is deleted, its three records, by the next session's save, in the same transaction as the new session, and the save tells how many it deleted; one a newer version wrote survives it.
- A refused keyed read still saves the new session and deletes nothing.
- A refused session save writes and deletes nothing, keeps the session in memory as the most recent one, and its keep line reads "Not saved on this device: export it to keep it."; a refusal for lack of space also raises the storage-full notice, and any other refusal raises no notice.
- The session's save returns its own outcome, saved, refused with its kind, or saving off, apart from the failure listener; the end is not delayed by it, and the keep line reads "Not pinned…" while it runs.
- While saving is off, a session's end reads, saves and deletes nothing, every kept session stays, and the keep line reads "Not saved on this device: export it to keep it."
- A session ended during its count-in changes nothing kept and leaves the most recent session in place.
- The most recent session stays in memory across the next Start until the next session ends with a review.
- After a reload, the most recent session is the readable kept session with the latest start, including when the unpinned one was deleted and only pinned ones remain; Review last session reads it by its key and opens the same review, notation and exports alike, and shows no review when the read is refused, its records are all absent or a newer version wrote them; damaged records are deleted and the list shows instead.
- In a session whose own save was refused, the next favorite, pin, unpin or comment saves it again under the same key with the facts held then; its played notes are kept only as decided at its end; on success the keep line takes its pinned or not-pinned wording, and a failed retry changes nothing shown.
- A session ended by reloading or closing the page is not saved: its save runs no transaction, the kept sessions stay as they were, and no notice shows.
- A save the page's leaving reaches while it runs (while the codec loads, or between the read and the batch) stops before its next transaction and resolves abandoned; it is never resumed or retried, even when the page is brought back from the back-forward cache, and its keep line reads "Not saved on this device: export it to keep it."
- After the page is brought back from the back-forward cache, the next session that ends with a review is saved, deleting the older unpinned kept session.
- A tab or app switch ends the session, and its save runs as before.
- At load, with saving on, every kept session whose summary is damaged is deleted in one batch run from the session controller's chunk, the entry chunk unchanged; the most recent session and the rows leave it out; the deletion is told with its count.
- At load, with no damaged summary among those the load read, the clean-up opens no transaction; when it runs, it reads the summaries again at the change: a summary damaged since the load is deleted with the others, and with none damaged at that read nothing is written or told.
- When the load's clean-up deletes the most recent session, a kept session not yet read into memory, the readable kept session that started last among the summaries its re-read found readable becomes the most recent at once, or there is none and review-link goes; a most recent session held in memory stays the most recent.
- At an opening, a session whose records are damaged is deleted at once in one batch and its row leaves; when it was the most recent session, the readable kept session that started last becomes the most recent at once.
- A read the device refuses, a session whose records are all absent and one a newer version wrote are never deleted, at load, at the save or at an opening; while saving is off nothing is deleted, and a damaged session found at an opening takes the failed-to-open path.
- A refused deletion of damaged sessions changes nothing stored, leaves those sessions unlisted and uncounted for the visit, and is told with its kind; a committed one is told with how many it deleted, and neither goes through the save policy's failure listener: the apology, Storage full and Damaged session not deleted all come from the deletion's report, through the channel the workspace builds and the workspace shell passes to the notices.
- A session this version saves is never taken for damaged: saved, reloaded and opened, its records are all still there, for each built-in instrument and my instrument.
- A retry of a session's own save that reaches its transaction while my instrument's batch counting that session is applied writes nothing; once the batch is saved, no retry writes that session; a refused batch lets the retry it held back run again.
