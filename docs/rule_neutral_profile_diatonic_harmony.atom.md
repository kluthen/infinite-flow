# Neutral Profile Diatonic Harmony

## INTENT
Keep neutral-profile harmonic motion diatonic to the selected tonal context.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
The neutral profile uses mostly diatonic chord movement. Low motion sustains tonic or tonic prolongation, and higher motion introduces diatonic departure and return; in tonal material active motion may progress through tonic, predominant, dominant, and an open dominant arrival. Modal material does not invent a raised leading tone solely to force a dominant cadence.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
The harmonic-motion control selects static, gentle diatonic, or active diatonic motion.

## EXPECTATION
Neutral-profile harmony is diatonic and modal material contains no invented raised leading tone used to force a dominant cadence.
