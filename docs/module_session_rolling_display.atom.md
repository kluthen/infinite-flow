# Session Rolling Display

## INTENT
Show a running practice session on the staff's two system positions as a rolling display: the phrase being played and the next one once it is ready, refilled one position at a time behind a playhead that steps per measure on the audio the player hears, and never a phrase that does not fit the staff layout held for the session.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
Placement plan. A pure plan, which reads no time and touches no page, decides what each of the two system positions shows from three inputs: the layout held at start, the playhead, and the highest phrase number readied so far (1 while only the Idle phrase is). A position shows one slot, a phrase number and a part (the whole phrase, bars 1-4; its opening, bars 1-2; or its closing, bars 3-4), or nothing. In the one-system layout phrase n lives in the top position when n is odd and in the bottom one when n is even; the phrase being played (phrase 1 during the count-in) shows in its position, and the other position shows the next phrase once it is readied, else nothing. In the split layout, while the playhead is in a phrase's measures 1-2 (or in the count-in, for phrase 1), the top position shows its opening and the bottom one its closing; from its measure 3 on, the bottom keeps its closing and the top shows the next phrase's opening once it is readied, else nothing. Waiting material is every part of every readied phrase, from the phrase being played on, that the positions do not show. Because the playhead runs on heard time and the engine on the audio context's time, the phrase after next can be readied while the heard playhead is still in the current phrase's last measure; it then waits, and is shown only once the playhead reaches the phrase before it. The position holding the playhead never changes content between two successive states: a position changes only after the playhead has left it. The plan also tells whether a given phrase is on screen, which phrases are shown or waiting, and how many measures after the one being played are on screen.

Playhead. The playhead is a phrase number and a measure index from 0 to 3, or none before phrase 1's first barline, during the count-in. Barline k lies at phrase 1's start time plus k measures, computed by multiplication and never by accumulation; exactly at a barline the playhead is on the new measure. It is read on heard time, the audio clock's time minus the audio output latency, once per frame through an injected frame loop (the browser's animation frames in the app, a fake in tests), and the display's state changes only when the position changes. The loop runs from the session's start until its end and is stopped at teardown. The display keeps the written phrase of every readied phrase from the playhead's phrase on, and forgets the ones the playhead has left.

Refill. When a position's slot changes, that position alone is refilled: the notes it held start fading out, and at that moment they lose every overlay mark and are hidden from assistive technology; the old engraving is removed once its fade has finished (at once when there is no animation, as under reduced motion), then the new slot, engraved off-screen first, enters with its own entrance fade. A blank slot leaves the position empty. A newer refill of a position supersedes an older one still under way. Re-engraving after a resize is not a refill: every slot shown is re-engraved at the new size in the held viewport (the held layout with the current window's height budget), in place and without a fade, all of them staged first and swapped in only if every one succeeds. Phrase 1's slot carries the very written phrase object Idle shows, not a copy, and entering the session with the slots Idle already shows at the same inputs redraws nothing, so the start never redraws phrase 1.

Fit. Every look-ahead phrase is checked, before it is readied, against the held viewport and the latest container width, with the intent of the attempt being checked, by the engraver's own staff planner, which the display loads through an injected loader once the engraver and its notation fonts are ready; a phrase that does not fit (notes too wide, or no room) is a failed attempt for the failure ladder and is never drawn. On every staff layout report during a session, after the session window rule has judged it, every phrase shown or waiting is checked again in the held viewport, and any misfit ends the session with the staff-not-shown cause, exactly like Stop.

Engraving outcomes. While the staff shows a session, no engraving outcome reaches the Idle machinery: the staff reports neither a successful engraving nor a notation error, only a session staff failure, for any typed notation error met while engraving the session (a layout failure, the engraver or its fonts failing to load, an engraving-library failure), which ends the session with the staff-not-shown cause. That failure passes through the same gate as the staff's other reports and goes only to the session diverting them; with no session diverting, it is dropped. A failure never shows the notation error state and never clears the staff. Entering or leaving the session display drops any engraving still in flight, so it reports nowhere. Leaving it removes at once the units still fading out and every overlay mark, the playhead band included, from the systems that stay on screen, which remain until the Idle engraving replaces or clears them; the staff then engraves the phrase Idle then shows, in the layout the window then selects, and only that engraving reports to Idle as usual. After an end during the count-in that is phrase 1 again. After an end once phrase 1 has sounded, Idle drops phrase 1 at once for the fresh phrase it generates, so the staff, kept laid out but out of sight under the last session's review, shows no phrase until that fresh phrase is drawn, and never again a phrase of the ended session.

Overlays and assistive text. Overlays are applied phrase by phrase, each on that phrase's systems on screen only, never on an engraving that is fading out. The playhead band is drawn on the measure being played, on the systems of the phrase that owns the playhead. No start marker is drawn during a session. A phrase's acknowledged notes are marked only when a system of that phrase on screen draws them; an acknowledged note of a part not on screen is left out. Each phrase on screen is described once to assistive technology, on the first of its systems shown, its other system hidden from it; the hidden description follows the playhead's phrase, phrase 1 during the count-in.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
The pure plan lives in `src/session/rolling-display.ts`: types `SystemSlot { phraseNumber, part: SystemPart }` (with `SystemPart = 'whole' | 'opening' | 'closing'` from the notation layout policy), `RollingSystems { top: SystemSlot | null, bottom: SystemSlot | null }` and `PlayheadPosition { phraseNumber, measureIndex: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 }`; functions `playheadAt(heardTime, firstPhraseStartsAt, measureSeconds): PlayheadPosition | null`, `systemsAt(layout, playhead | null, latestReady): RollingSystems`, `waitingMaterial(layout, playhead | null, latestReady): SystemSlot[]`, `phrasesShownOrWaiting(layout, playhead | null, latestReady): number[]`, `readAheadMeasures(layout, playhead, latestReady)`, `isPhraseOnScreen(layout, playhead | null, latestReady, n): boolean` and `slotKey(slot)`. PhraseScore takes an optional `session: SessionStaff | null` (default null), with `SessionStaff = { layout, top, bottom, playhead }`, `top` and `bottom` each a `SessionSystem | null`, and `SessionSystem = SystemSlot & { phrase: WrittenPhrase }` (`src/components/notation/session-staff.ts`; the positions' DOM keeper is `session-staff-positions.ts`, the marks `session-staff-marks.ts`); in session mode it emits only `session-staff-failed` with a NotationError, never `engraved` or `notation-error`, and each `staff-content` unit carries `data-phrase-number` and `data-system-part`. The Idle layout gate's `SessionStaffReports` adds `sessionStaffFailed(error)` to the staff's reports. The controller side, `createSessionDisplay` in `src/components/workspace/workspace-session-display.ts`, keeps the readied written phrases, the latest readied phrase number and the playhead, drives the playhead through the `frames: { request(cb): number; cancel(handle): void }` port of the controller's clock dependencies, and supplies the runner's fit check through the controller's `staffFitPlanner: () => Promise<StaffFitPlanner>` dependency (production: `loadSessionStaffFitPlanner` in `src/components/workspace/session-staff-fixed-parts.ts`). The controller state exposes reactive `sessionStaff: SessionStaff | null` and `latestReady: number | null`, both null outside a session, and the controller an `isPhraseOnScreen(n)` query, false outside a session.

## EXPECTATION
- During the count-in there is no playhead; from phrase 1's first barline the playhead moves one measure at each barline of the heard audio, and exactly at a barline it is on the new measure.
- The position holding the playhead never changes content between two successive states.
- In the one-system layout odd phrases show in the top position and even ones in the bottom; in the split layout the top position takes the next phrase's opening from the current phrase's measure 3.
- A phrase readied beyond what the positions show waits, and is shown only once the playhead reaches the phrase before it.
- A refill touches only its own position: fade-out, with the fading notes unmarked and hidden from assistive technology at once, then the new slot's entrance; a resize re-engraves the shown slots in place with no fade.
- A look-ahead phrase that does not fit the held viewport is never drawn and counts as a failed attempt.
- A resize that leaves a shown or waiting phrase misfit, or a session staff failure, ends the session with the staff-not-shown cause, like Stop; a session staff failure with no session running reaches nothing.
- Starting a session redraws nothing, and no engraving outcome during a session reaches the Idle machinery.
- During a session no start marker is drawn, the band shows only on the playhead's phrase, and only acknowledged notes drawn on screen are marked; at the end the band leaves at once and the staff engraves the Idle phrase in the window's layout: phrase 1 after an end during the count-in, and after an end once phrase 1 has sounded no phrase of the ended session, only the fresh phrase Idle generates.
