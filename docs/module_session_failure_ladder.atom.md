# Session Failure Ladder

## INTENT
Turn every failed look-ahead attempt into a bounded series of retries and, when no valid phrase exists at the deadline, a repeat of the phrase before, so no failure ever ends the session.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
One attempt per look-ahead phrase is in flight at a time. A failed attempt is a generation that found no phrase, a rejected generate request (a worker crash or a dead worker), or a phrase that does not fit. The rungs are a first carry-in attempt, up to three carry-in retries with a fresh seed and the same carry-in, and one standalone retry with no carry-in and a fresh seed, its register fixed to the session's resolved register window so a Varying register stays resolved once per session. A phrase whose carry-in a settings change left unusable climbs the same rungs, every attempt standalone.

The ladder functions stay pure and time-free: whether the standalone attempt is due, the look-ahead timing's test for the event time against the phrase's deadline, reaches them as a boolean computed by the engine. The first rung of a phrase, when it is requested and when a settings change drops its attempt to request it again, is the first carry-in rung, or the standalone rung when the standalone attempt is already due. The rung after a failure is decided by a pure function of the failed rung, the failure and that boolean: when the standalone attempt is due, a failed carry-in attempt goes to the standalone rung; otherwise a failed carry-in attempt whose generation failure code is no_legal_pitch_plan goes straight to the standalone rung, since for a carry-in request that code means exactly that its one window was searched to exhaustion (no fuel cutoff, no validation failure), and the seed only orders candidates, so every carry-in retry would fail the same way; every other failure, a search_limit_exceeded fuel cutoff, a result validation failure, a crash and a failed fit check included, goes to the next rung. Only the outcome's failure code is read, never its diagnostics. The cutover rung is the standalone rung for an attempt whose step is carry-in and which is still in its generating stage when the standalone attempt is due, and none for any other attempt.

After every event is handled, the engine runs the cutover for the open look-ahead phrase: a carry-in attempt still generating at or after the phrase's cutover time is cancelled, the engine outputting cancel generation for its attempt id, and the standalone attempt is requested at once, in that order within the event's outputs. Every carry-in request also outputs a wake-at at its phrase's cutover time, so the cutover runs on time even when no other event arrives. A carry-in attempt whose successful result arrives at or after the cutover time, before the cutover has run, reaches its fit check and is kept; if that check fails, the next rung is the standalone one. Whenever carry-in rungs are skipped, by the no_legal_pitch_plan skip, by a first rung that is already standalone, by a carry-in failure once the standalone attempt is due, or by the cutover, the engine draws and discards the seeds and request ids those rungs would have used, so the standalone intent is identical to the one built after three failed carry-in retries.

A successful outcome becomes ready only after its fit check passes. If no valid phrase exists at the deadline, whether the ladder is exhausted or an attempt is still in flight, the previous phrase is repeated as the next one, decided at the deadline and not earlier. The phrase after a standalone or a repeat carries in normally from that record. When the deadline decides a repeat while an attempt is still in its generating stage, the engine cancels that attempt, outputting cancel generation for it, so the next phrase's first request never waits behind it on the one generation worker; an attempt already waiting for its fit check is not cancelled, since its generation is done. A cancelled attempt, at the cutover or at the deadline, is a dropped attempt, not a failed one: it climbs no rung by failing. Results for an unknown, superseded or cancelled attempt, or arriving after the deadline or the end, are dropped; after the end, ticks and controller events are dropped too. No failure ever ends the session.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
The engine's request-generation output carries `step` (carry-in or standalone) and `retry`, the rung number (0 for the first carry-in attempt, 1 to 3 for the carry-in retries, 4 for the standalone retry, also when carry-in retries were skipped or cut over); a repeat is a `SessionPhrase` with origin `repeat` and `repeatOf`. The rung ladder and the pure rung functions are in `src/session/failure-ladder.ts`: `firstRung(standaloneDue: boolean)`, `rungAfterFailure(failed, failure, standaloneDue: boolean)` and `cutoverRung(attempt, standaloneDue: boolean)`, the last returning the standalone rung only for an attempt with `step` carry-in in its `generating` stage, and undefined otherwise. The engine's ladder steps are in `src/session/session-ladder-step.ts`: `requestFirstRung`, the first rung of a phrase; `climb`, which reads only the generation outcome's `code`, computes the boolean with `standaloneDue(at, deadline)` from `src/session/session-timing.ts`, and draws and discards the skipped rungs' seeds and request ids; `runCutover`, which `src/session/session-engine.ts` runs after each event's dispatch; the rung request with its `wake-at` at each carry-in request's `cutoverAt(deadline)`; and `cancelDropped`, the `cancel-generation { attemptId }` output for an attempt still generating when it is cut over, and, called from the engine, when the deadline decides a repeat. The phrase being looked ahead for, not yet decided, with its deadline and the attempt in flight, if any (none once the ladder is exhausted), is `LookAhead` in `src/session/failure-ladder.ts`.

## EXPECTATION
Mixed generation failures, crashes and failed fit checks run up to three carry-in retries, then one standalone retry keeping the resolved register, before the deadline; with no valid phrase at the deadline the previous phrase is repeated, marked as a repeat of the phrase directly before, and the session keeps running. A carry-in attempt failing with no_legal_pitch_plan is followed directly by the standalone retry (retry 4); a carry-in attempt failing with search_limit_exceeded or result_validation_failed, a crash or a failed fit check is followed by the next carry-in retry while the standalone attempt is not due, and by the standalone retry once it is. A phrase requested when the standalone attempt is already due gets the standalone retry as its first attempt. A carry-in attempt still generating at its phrase's cutover time is output as cancel generation, followed in the same outputs by the standalone retry's request; a carry-in success arriving after the cutover time but before the cutover ran is fit-checked and kept if it fits. Every carry-in request is accompanied by a wake-at at its cutover time. However the standalone retry is reached, its intent, seed and request id equal those it would have had after three failed carry-in retries. An attempt still generating when the deadline decides a repeat is cancelled before the next request is made, and one waiting for its fit check is not. A late, superseded or cancelled attempt's result never becomes a phrase.
