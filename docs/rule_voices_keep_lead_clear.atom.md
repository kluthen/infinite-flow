# Every Voice's Sound Keeps the Lead Clear

## INTENT
Let the player hear their own line clearly through everything the app plays.

## THE RULE / LOGIC
Each system voice's sound keeps the lead clear by its register, its brightness and its attack, away from the melody guide's pure, quick-starting tone at the lead's own pitch. The highlighting part blends with the lead it doubles, which is its purpose, but starts softer than the melody guide.

## TECHNICAL INTERFACE
Each voice's sound is a fixed choice of the app, set by ear.

## EXPECTATION
- No part sounds with the melody guide's pure, quick-starting tone.
- The highlighting part starts softer than the melody guide.
